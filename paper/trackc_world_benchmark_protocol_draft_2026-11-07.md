# Track C independent-world benchmark: draft (2026-11-07)

**Status: development draft; not frozen or authorized for a sealed run.** The project title remains “Integrating Tumor Biology and Longitudinal MRI for Glioblastoma Progression Modeling and In-Silico Therapy Optimization.” No outcomes are generated by this draft.

## Question and estimand

Under declared simulated signal and tumor-response assumptions, how does the fixed finite-set dependence-aware minimax policy compare with its matched finite-set `d=0` policy? For each simulated patient, calculate the candidate's utility minus the matched control's utility. Utility is the smaller of baseline-referenced and RANO-like progression times, each restricted at 730 days, less five days per treatment course through day 730. Average paired patient differences within each independently generated world, then average world means equally. Positive differences favor the candidate. This utility and its penalty are modeling choices, not clinical endpoints or patient preferences.

The two finite-set controllers must use the same `q/a/b` support, patient inputs, scan schedule, treatment options, course cap, and prediction settings. Their signal-dependence support is the intended difference. The LOO dependence-aware versus LOO independence comparison is a separate descriptive contrast: LOO weights use other evaluation patients' baseline signals. Both finite-set policies are currently exploratory controllers in the benchmark, and this document does not promote them to an adopted primary endpoint.

## Design before outcomes

1. Generate separate selection, calibration, and evaluation patients per world. Reserve distinct scenario, signal, policy, and mixture seeds across all streams and worlds; audit the seed registry before recording the final allocation. Record ordered patient IDs and fingerprints, code and environment, simulation settings, and input hashes. Distinct hashes and seeds are checks, not proof of independence.
2. Use the proposed `observed_two_scan` treatment stop for the main development design. It is a synthetic noisy-volume proxy, not formal RANO. Record latent progression outcomes on each policy's own treatment trajectory; do not use hidden progression to stop treatment in this design.
3. Include at least the matched `d=0` minimax, no-signal MPC, selected simple scan rule, fixed schedules, and the separate-cohort equal-expected-course schedule control. Preserve every comparator's result. Report the realized course difference; equal expected courses do not imply patient-level dose matching.
4. Predeclare signal-reliability and dependence anchors, including `d=0` and values outside the controller's fitted grid. Predeclare response-family and model-mismatch scenarios. Report every anchor separately; do not pool heterogeneous anchors merely to obtain a favorable mean.
5. Declare simulated `miss_p` and, where applicable, `miss_p_hi`, separately from the MPC's assumed future scan-loss probability. Nonzero scan loss in the shared benchmark requires `miss_visible` to be an all-true Boolean patient array. Include complete-follow-up and visible-missing-scan cases, plus a state-linked mismatch case. Specify numerical probabilities before any outcome run.
6. Before outcome scoring, fix world counts, patients per cohort, exact anchors, fresh seed blocks, the minimum useful difference, and precision targets using a timing-only or outcome-blind pilot. Fix the interval procedure and multiplicity family in the same amendment. A percentile bootstrap over independent world means is implemented as a prototype, but is not yet selected as the final inference method. Avoid treating patients nested within a world as independent worlds.
7. Report per-world and per-anchor paired utilities, both progression measures, treatment courses, censoring, and uncertainty. Report ties, missing arms, failed runs, and harms. Never alter settings or select a subset after seeing held-out outcomes. A failed precision target yields an inconclusive result, not a pass.

## Feasibility and implementation gates

The existing timing-only pilot took 47.60 minutes for two small worlds, each with two patients in each cohort; it did not measure policy outcomes. This is not a reliable runtime estimate for the proposed study ([timing record](trackc_robust_method_research_and_timing_protocol_2026-11-06.md)). Benchmark `src/211_trackc_policy_benchmark.py` runs one world; `src/trackc_world_summary.py` can summarize declared LOO or minimax contrasts from compatible records. A research driver that reserves and audits fresh world seeds, enforces disjoint cohorts, runs all predeclared anchors, records failures, and verifies complete output is still needed. Fingerprint checks do not prove world independence or patient pairing.

Before any outcome run: implement and test the driver with fake records, fix the exact settings and resource limit, register fresh seeds, and record a dated protocol and code seal. The existing sealed-run rule remains in force. If the resources or precision target cannot be met, report the study as infeasible or inconclusive rather than reducing the planned evidence after viewing outcomes.

## Interpretation

The molecular-to-resistance relationship is an assumed simulator link. Track A expression and Duke MRI are not matched within patients. Even a positive result would support only the stated synthetic comparison under its assumptions, not a verified resistance biomarker, a clinical drug recommendation, or superiority over every existing method. A negative or mixed result is equally reportable.
