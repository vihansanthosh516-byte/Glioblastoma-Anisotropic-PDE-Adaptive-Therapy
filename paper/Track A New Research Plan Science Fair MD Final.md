# Track A New — Proposed Malignant-Cell Biology Research Plan

**Status:** Proposed study only; not ready to run. No qualified discovery/validation cohort pair or new malignant-cell signature exists. No primary endpoint or success rule is frozen. This plan does not amend A5 or authorize dataset access or expression analysis.

## Rationale

Glioblastoma varies across core and infiltrative regions. Pai et al. (2025) compared malignant tumor cells from matched Core and Margin dissections and defined a 153-gene Margin-up “glioma infiltration” signature. Harwood et al. (2025) profiled eight patients using a targeted 1,000-gene CosMx panel and reported patient-variable malignant-state shifts across core and infiltrated regions. Other studies describe regional programs and invasion-associated states (Yu et al., 2020; Darmanis et al., 2017; Wang et al., 2024; Sonpatki et al., 2026; Chanoch-Myers et al., 2026; Mangukiya et al., 2026). Core-margin differences, malignant infiltration programs, and regional state shifts are established findings. The higher-potential Track A New design will discover a malignant-cell program that persists within established cell states, compare it with the published Pai signature, and validate the locked new program in an untouched cohort. This could support a new, replicated regional association; it cannot guarantee a new mechanism or positive result.

This plan aims for a donor-level, controlled biological association. It will not establish causation, a new mechanism, treatment response, or clinical utility.

## Research Question

Can a donor-reproducible malignant-cell expression program specific to the infiltrative Margin be discovered within established malignant-cell states, beyond changes in state proportions and the published Pai margin signature, and then independently validated in at least six new IDH-wildtype GBM donors with matched Core and Margin samples?

## Hypothesis

The discovery stage will identify a candidate Margin-up expression program within malignant-cell states that is stable across discovery donors and adds information beyond the locked Pai signature. After that program, state definitions, and fixed standardization weights are locked, its equal-donor Margin-minus-Core score will remain positive in an untouched cohort of at least six new IDH-wildtype donors. The primary analysis will use validated malignant cells. State-proportion differences and sensitivity to non-malignant-cell fraction will be reported separately. The numerical success and precision rule remains open until a dated amendment; neither outcome will be interpreted as causal.

## Procedure

1. **Keep A5 separate.** A5 remains a completed mixed-cell prediction study with MRI-marked sampling-point labels. Its research question, cohort, protocol, result, and figure remain unchanged. A5’s failed classifier does not count as biological evidence for this question and will not supply a malignant-cell signature.
2. **Pass every cohort gate using public metadata only.** Broad scouting is paused. Before any data or expression access, a named discovery source and a separate validation accession must have no-request public access, clear dataset-specific reuse terms, exact donor/sample/region joins, per-case IDH1/2 evidence, and defensible cell-level malignant labels with documented validation. The untouched validation cohort must retain at least six previously unused IDH-wildtype GBM donors after exclusions, with matched Core and infiltrative-Margin samples per donor, enough malignant cells in each prespecified state and region, compatible assay coverage for the locked program, and verified nonoverlap with the local donor manifest, prior-use audits, and discovery/signature cohorts. The discovery cohort size must have a donor-level precision rationale; six or more donors is preferred, and larger samples are better. Article and dataset terms are separate gates. Spatial spots alone are not malignant-cell labels. Any unknown gate stops the study.
3. **Use the existing ledger and named leads without treating them as approved.** No screened validation cohort passes every gate. GSE286419 has four retained Core/Margin pairs. GSE318564 has three explicit IDH-wildtype Core/Edge pairs and an unresolved annotation join. SNUH-GliomaAtlas has six pairs but overlaps prior project use. HRA009021 is a named metadata-only discovery lead: its paper reports 26 IDH-wildtype GBM patients and a 10-person, 30-tissue single-cell subset from that study, supporting IDH-wildtype status for that subset at the paper-cohort level. An existing [metadata-only audit](trackA_v2_data_check.md) links the 10 GSA donor codes to the paper’s donor-coded HBI/LBI/Tumor labels, but not individual GSA sample accessions to regions. The official [GSA-Human record](https://ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/gsa-human/browse/HRA009021) marks access Open. The [NGDC access policy](https://ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/gsa-human/document/Principle_of_Accessing_Human_Genetic_Resource_Data_in_NGDC_V1.pdf) permits open-access use for research, restricts use to the stated research scope and research group/collaborators, prohibits redistribution and participant identification, and allows submitters to set specific restrictions; no accession-specific restriction or license is displayed. The official [sample record](https://ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/gsa-human/browse/sampleDetail/HRS1407319) maps GBM2301 / HRS1407319 to individual HRI1073636 and lists related sample records HRS1407320 and HRS1407321; it gives no region. Therefore, exact sample-to-region assignment, accession-to-paper case mapping, ready-to-use validated malignant-cell labels, any accession-specific reuse restrictions, and patient-level independence remain unresolved. Its paper already studies regional tumor-cell differences; it could be a discovery source only if every gate passes, not the untouched validation source. Harwood Zenodo 22013127 / concept record 16957940 remains unconfirmed: donor/sample mapping, per-case IDH status, FOV-region mapping, annotation fields and validation, and patient-level overlap are unresolved. Its dataset-record license is separate from the article license. The paper studies core-versus-infiltrated differences and uses targeted CosMx. The ZIP scope deviation is documented in the [Track A dataset plan](trackA_alternate_dataset_plan_20261101.md). Broad scouting remains paused.
4. **Freeze the discovery and validation procedures before expression access.** A dated amendment must name both sources, the new-signature selection procedure, the exact gene-level criteria, normalization, required gene coverage, locked malignant-state labels, reference source and state weights, region mapping, cell-count exclusions, one donor-level primary endpoint, effect direction, meaningful-effect and precision criteria, success rule, multiplicity plan, and permitted data workflow. The discovery procedure will be fitted only in discovery donors. The resulting signature and analysis settings will be locked before validation expression is opened. No validation data may be used to choose genes, states, weights, thresholds, or model settings. The published Pai signature will be a fixed comparator, not a substitute discovery result.
5. **Map samples, regions, and malignant cells from source metadata.** Join each cell to its sample, donor, region, and malignant label using documented identifiers. Confirm per-case IDH status and label provenance and validation. Count exclusions and conflicts. Do not infer labels from expression, file order, suffixes, or clusters; do not substitute spatial spots for malignant cells. Stop if any mapping or label gate is unclear.
6. **Build paired donor-level measurements.** Aggregate validated malignant-cell counts by donor, region, and prespecified malignant-cell state into pseudobulk measurements. Donors will be the independent units and cells will be subsamples, consistent with sample-level differential-state methods (Crowell et al., 2020; Squair et al., 2021; Zimmerman et al., 2021). Use identical region and state definitions across discovery and validation. Measure state proportions and the sample-level fraction of non-malignant cells where source annotations support them.
7. **Discover and lock the within-state program.** In discovery donors, identify Margin-up genes within prespecified malignant states using the donor-level procedure in the amendment. Require a stability rule across discovery donors and document the resulting gene list, weights, score formula, and coverage before validation. Compare the new program with the published Pai signature and state-matched control gene sets. Do not choose genes or thresholds from validation donors.
8. **Test once in the untouched cohort and report every donor.** Apply the locked gene list, state weights, score method, and preprocessing once. Report each donor’s paired difference, equal-donor estimate, uncertainty, exclusions, state-standardized result, and sensitivity to normal-brain content. Use the prespecified success and precision criteria. Do not treat cells as independent patients or add donors after seeing results.
9. **Interpret both positive and negative outcomes.** A persistent signal supports a replicated within-malignant-cell regional association. A signal that disappears after state standardization indicates that the total contrast tracked state proportions under this design. A failed or imprecise result does not establish equivalence or absence. Pathway enrichment may be shown only as exploratory, with multiple-testing correction. Keep any future result separate from the current figure.

## Data Analysis

The primary endpoint will be each validation donor’s paired Margin-minus-Core difference in the locked new-signature score, calculated within malignant-cell states and combined with fixed weights derived only from discovery/reference data. The primary summary will be the equal-donor mean with a donor-level uncertainty interval. The dated amendment must define the discovery procedure, exact signature score, region and state definitions, fixed weights, direction, precision and meaningful-effect thresholds, exclusions, and multiplicity rule before validation expression is opened. Secondary results will show the unstandardized new-signature contrast, the fixed Pai-signature comparison, state-proportion changes, and sensitivity to sample non-malignant-cell fraction. Donors are the independent units; cells are subsamples (Crowell et al., 2020; Squair et al., 2021; Zimmerman et al., 2021). Six donors are the eligibility floor, not a guarantee of adequate precision.

## Stop Rules

Stop before expression access if there is no qualifying discovery source and separate validation source; fewer than six independent matched validation donors remain; any case lacks accession-linked IDH1/2 evidence; donor/sample/region mapping fails; malignant labels lack source-backed validation; reuse terms are unclear; overlap with the project or signature-discovery cohorts cannot be ruled out; too few signature genes are measured; too few malignant cells support a required state/region score; or assay/region definitions cannot be harmonized. Stop the new-signature claim if the program is unstable across discovery donors, the locked program does not add information beyond the Pai comparator under the frozen rule, or the signature, endpoint, weights, or success rule changes after validation is opened. A published-signature replication requires a separate scope amendment.

## Novelty Boundary

Pai et al. (2025) already reported malignant-cell Core/Margin expression differences and a 153-gene Margin-up signature; Harwood et al. (2025) reported region-dependent malignant-state proportions in targeted spatial data. Therefore, Track A New will not claim first discovery of margin biology, and the Pai signature itself will not be presented as new. Its higher-potential contribution is a new, discovery-derived within-state program that adds information beyond the published signature and replicates in an untouched IDH-wildtype cohort. That remains a proposal: no qualifying discovery/validation pair currently passes the gates, and success would support a regional association rather than a mechanism or causal effect.
