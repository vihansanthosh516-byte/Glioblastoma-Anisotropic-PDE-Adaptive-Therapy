# Track A failure audit

## A5 region classification

A5 failed because its lower mean class-balanced AURC did not translate into the required donor-level consistency. It won in four of six donors against logistic regression, below the frozen five-of-six rule. The lowest mean does not override that rule. Source: [frozen protocol](trackA_yu2020/PROTOCOL.md) and [saved result](../output/trackA_yu2020/result.json).

The candidate's mixed-cell advantage did not carry into the eligible within-Myeloid check, where performance was near chance. The composition-only control was close to the best expression classifier. These descriptive controls make composition a plausible contributor. They cannot identify why particular donors lost or establish whether malignant-cell expression carries regional information. Labels apply to MRI-marked sampling points and the cohort is small. Source: [saved result](../output/trackA_yu2020/result.json) and [review follow-up](trackA_review_followup_2026-11-10.md).

## TMZ

The exploratory regional score had split donor directions, and its expression contrast could not measure drug response. The later fixed-score HGCC bridge used measured viability AUC. Its Spearman estimate was −0.102, with bootstrap interval −0.327 to 0.128, and the score trailed the median matched random panel. This closes the tested proxy route. The assay, platform, and culture conditions differed and some signature genes could not be measured. These are plausible transfer limits, with no experiment isolating their effects. The bridge is secondary reanalysis of a previously published assay and HGCC is now spent. Sources: [regional amendment and result](trackA_tmz_response_119of121_amendment_2026-11-08.md), [HGCC result](../output/trackA_hgcc_tmz_102/result.json), and [HGCC interpretation](trackA_hgcc_tmz_102_result_2026-11-12.md).
