Sources include [GBM-Space](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-11234-8), [Proseg](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-025-02697-0), [SPLIT](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-026-03089-8), [Points2Regions](https://doi.org/10.1002/cyto.a.24884), [Ntafoulis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10575865/), [Ju](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11700395/), [PERCEPTION](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38637658/) and [CaDRReS-Sc](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/pmid/34915921/). See also the [broader comparator inventory](trackA_prior_art_full_2026-11-16.md).

For the regional development check, order each pair by specimen ID before revealing region labels. In each reconstruction, take the fitted spatial-plus-nonspatial margin and its interval under the independently calibrated shared-error bound. Require every interval to lie strictly on the same side of zero. Fall back to the nonspatial decision when the intervals disagree in sign, include zero, or lack a valid bound. Establish the bound on independent reference annotations and measure whether it covers the margin error in held-out development sections. Report the fallback rate and the severity of uncovered errors. Earlier tests identified failure modes for [agreement](trackA_segmentation_result.md), [positive-control mismatch](trackA_probe_mismatch_result.md) and [partial artifact coverage](trackA_assay_transport_result.md).

For TMZ, weight training origins equally in the mean loss. Center both expression and outcome within each repeated training origin and average its centered loss before combining origins. Singletons contribute to the mean loss only. A within-origin mean-centered prediction sums to zero, so a wholly separate within-origin coefficient vector cannot directly change mean predictions when those predictions are aggregated within origin. Shared coefficients make the comparison interpretable at culture and origin levels. Score each external origin once for the primary endpoint and predeclare how to evaluate culture contrasts among paired origins. Specify penalty, within-origin weight and dose harmonization only after the eligible data structure is clear.
