# Track A segmentation agreement falsification

**Simulation. Agreement failed the locked common-error check.** Paired concordance was 0.50 without spatial in each constructed regime. The agreement rule scored 1.00 with signal, 0.50 with divergent errors, and 0.00 with shared errors. The loss follows directly from the margins: adding the shared negative spatial term reverses the positive baseline case and keeps the negative one wrong. [Exact result](../output/trackA_segmentation/result.json) | [Frozen design](trackA_segmentation_plan.md)

Reconstructing one section multiple ways is a sensitivity check for segmentation-specific effects. It is insufficient to detect errors inherited from the same molecule calls or image. A credible spatial addition needs an independent source of evidence, such as registered orthogonal markers and positive controls for contamination, or a prospectively validated bound on common error. If an externally calibrated bound leaves the corrected paired margin straddling zero, retain the nonspatial decision. The bound can limit the specific errors it was calibrated to cover.
