# Research-paper strengthening handoff

Research completed November 16, 2026. This is literature and dataset screening only. No new track or protocol was adopted; no patient values, reserved outcomes, or policy results were analyzed. START_HERE.md remains authoritative, and all existing evaluation gates remain unchanged.

The full report is [GBM paper strengthening options](../reports/GBM%20paper%20strengthening%20options.md). Source-linked research notes cover [biology](../research_notes/GBM%20paper%20strengthening%20options/biology.md), [imaging](../research_notes/GBM%20paper%20strengthening%20options/imaging.md), and [control/integration](../research_notes/GBM%20paper%20strengthening%20options/control.md).

The strongest near-term control extension is independent-mechanism falsification of EARF, explicitly separating world-generating assumptions from controller assumptions. It remains simulation-only and inherits the current Track C run and precision gates. Generic sensitivity, identifiability, robust control, and value-of-information analyses already exist in the project and literature.

The strongest new biological candidates are patient-paired recurrence-state robustness using GSE174554 and orthogonal spatial RNA-to-protein validation using GSE237183/Zenodo 8105466. Next work is metadata-only: reconcile 36 analyzed pairs versus the GEO listing of 40 pairs, establish origin-level independence, and verify RNA/CODEX specimen/section joins. Neither candidate has a verified eligible cohort yet.

The most direct MRI addition is empirical measurement error propagated into frozen forecast comparisons. RIDER-NEURO-MRI lists 19 repeat-scan patients but currently points to NIH controlled image access; do not advertise it as unrestricted or ready. No open multi-rater longitudinal GBM substitute passed eligibility here. CFB/WAW remain protected by the locked Track B protocol; spent cohorts remain spent.

Retrieval limits: key biological papers were read in full, but several official dataset records and control references remained indexed/search/abstract-level. A Firecrawl retrieval/feedback batch stalled and was interrupted; fallback web searches completed for biology and imaging. Counts, access terms, sample joins, and closest-method novelty require targeted verification before selecting a protocol. Effort estimates in the report are planning judgments, not runtime measurements or publication guarantees.


Metadata follow-up: [recurrence gate](recurrence_metadata_gate_2026-11-16.md) now uses official GEO text metadata. The official summary really claims 40 pairs, but actual snRNA records contain 27 simple paired IDs plus three both-stage groups needing duplicate/multiple-recurrence resolution; usable pairs remain conditional. [Spatial gate](spatial_protein_metadata_gate_2026-11-16.md) verifies open CC BY 4.0 release 12624860 and five donor-label overlaps, but no complete section crosswalk. RNA/CODEX are near-adjacent sections. Prefer recurrence metadata reconciliation next; keep spatial validation conditional/exploratory. No new protocol is adopted.
