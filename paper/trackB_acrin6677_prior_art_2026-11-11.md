# ACRIN forecast prior-art check (2026-11-11)

Searched public indexes for ACRIN central-reader progression and volumetry, glioblastoma MRI growth forecasting under bevacizumab, Swanson proliferation–invasion modeling, and Claret tumor growth inhibition. Reviewed indexed primary-study descriptions.

- [Boxerman central-reader study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23788270/) related early enhancement progression to overall survival. [The delta-T1 substudy](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31248863/) compared imaging delineation methods and examined survival associations.
- [Schmainda et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4490871/) studied perfusion markers for survival. [Ellingson et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4383029/) studied diffusion quality control and functional diffusion maps in the same trial.
- [Swanson et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18059395/) used pretreatment MRI and proliferation–invasion modeling to predict glioblastoma growth and survival. [Claret et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19636014/) modeled longitudinal tumor size with growth and treatment effects in colorectal cancer.

The retrieved descriptions did not establish whether anyone ran this exact next-visit area comparison. Full-text coverage, forward citation tracing, and an exhaustive search of antiangiogenic forecasting remain unverified. No novelty claim.
