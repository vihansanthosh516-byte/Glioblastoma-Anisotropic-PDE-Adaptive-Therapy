# Track A parallel research directions

Keep the core-versus-infiltrating region and measured TMZ response endpoints. These are candidate designs following earlier negative results. No new test data were opened. See [recovery plan](trackA_recovery_plan.md) and [existing prior art](trackA_prior_art_full_2026-11-16.md).

## Spatial evidence beyond composition and pairwise contacts

Lead candidate: measure how much rearrangement of malignant state labels is needed to erase the predicted region difference. Start with a fixed statistic describing malignant cells connected through a myeloid neighbor. Compare the native map to rearrangements that preserve the tissue graph, state counts, and first-order contact totals. Allow swaps only within predefined depth and density strata. A verified swap sequence provides an upper bound on the smallest disruption budget. Claim an exact minimum only when exhaustive search or a certified bound establishes it. Report cases with no feasible rearrangement or poor search coverage separately.

First falsification: construct synthetic tissue graphs with identical composition and first-order contact counts but different degree of grouping along these paths. Include a nuisance-only null with sampling depth imbalance. If the constraints make valid rearrangements too rare or the score follows the nuisance, stop and diagnose. Future held-out patients need cell coordinates, independent region labels, and frozen cell annotations. Compare against expression and composition alone, geometry alone, pairwise enrichment, higher-order motifs, and a variant without constrained rearrangement. Use the same patients and paired concordance. Report disruption budget separately as a sensitivity measurement. ## Separate the assay artifact from the regional signal

Use randomized processing of aliquots from the same tissue or RNA to estimate how assay conditions move expression. Learn the covariance of those shifts on training controls. Fit a paired classifier whose margin is penalized for sensitivity to the measured shifts. Compare against paired logistic, control-derived nuisance projection, a diagonal penalty, and shuffled replicate links. Keep a whole acquisition condition out of development. Stress-test overlapping biological and artifact directions. If they cannot be distinguished, a classifier may discard genuine biology. Read counts split in software are inadequate substitutes for independent processing. [RUV control replicates](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkz433)

## Use within-origin comparisons to learn TMZ response

Ask whether an untreated expression model can learn measured viability differences among independently derived sister cultures while retaining between-origin predictive information. Fit both a within-origin contrast loss and an origin-mean loss. Evaluate on untouched origins and preferably a separate lab. Compare against the origin-mean loss alone, MGMT, regularized expression, and a fixed published signature. Shuffle sister links as a falsification check. Require a verified origin/specimen/assay join, measured TMZ curves, untreated expression, and independent sister cultures. Technical wells cannot stand in for those cultures. The MRI-guided multisampling paper is a candidate data lead pending a metadata check of endpoint coverage and eligibility.
