# Track A Research Plan — Science Fair MD Final

**Scope:** This submission covers Track A A5 only: the frozen Yu 2020 Core-versus-Periphery prediction study. Track A New is a separate proposed biology study and is not part of this plan.

**Status note:** A5 is complete and frozen. The sections below state its protocol in research-plan form; they do not imply that the analysis remains unperformed. The completed result and limits are recorded in the [A5 plan and result record](Track%20A5%20Research%20Plan%20Science%20Fair%20MD%20Final.md).

## Research Question

Will a sparse, donor-balanced expression classifier improve donor-level Core-versus-Periphery prediction over ordinary logistic regression when every test donor is excluded from training?

## Hypothesis

The candidate classifier will achieve a lower mean donor-level class-balanced area under the risk-coverage curve (AURC) than ordinary L2 logistic regression. It will also achieve lower AURC in at least five of the six primary donors. This is a prediction hypothesis, not a claim about malignant-cell biology or causation.

## Procedure

The following steps describe the completed, frozen A5 workflow. The cohort, labels, model settings, endpoints, and success rule were not changed after held-out scoring.

1. **Freeze and verify the cohort and sources.** A5 used the public Yu et al. 2020 single-cell RNA-sequencing count matrix (GEO GSE117891), the GEO cell-to-sampling-point table, Yu et al.'s Table S1 point labels, and a GBmap Core `annotation_level_2` metadata snapshot for the cell-type diagnostic only. The frozen primary donor list was GS1, GS2, GS3, GS5, GS6, and GS13. Cohort eligibility followed the frozen GBM-histology and recorded IDH1 R132 field rule; that field does not establish comprehensive IDH1/2-wildtype status. GS11 and GS12 were defined only for the sensitivity cohort, which was not run.
2. **Map sampling points, label regions, and apply cell quality control.** Exact barcode, point, and patient identifiers were used to connect GEO cells to Table S1. This mapped 75 labeled sampling points with zero unmapped target points and zero unlisted target points. `Tumoral` points defined Core, `Peritumoral` points defined Periphery, and `Normal` points were excluded. These were author-supplied MRI-marked sampling-point labels, not histology labels for individual cells. The frozen quality filters retained cells with at least 1,000 UMIs and 500 detected genes. All 4,139 matched target cells passed; 2,519 were from the six scored donors. The run excluded 54 Normal-point cells and 1,955 cells from other patients. Each primary donor had at least 20 cells in each class; GS5 had the smallest Periphery class, with 35 cells. No region label was inferred from expression or file order.
3. **Preprocess within the training donors.** In each training fold, genes detected in at least 5% of training cells were retained, the 2,000 most variable training genes were selected, each cell was normalized to 10,000 counts and transformed with `log1p`, and features were standardized using training-cell means and standard deviations. The fitted transform was applied unchanged to validation and held-out test cells.
4. **Evaluate with donors held out.** The primary analysis used six outer leave-one-donor-out folds and nested leave-one-training-donor-out tuning. All cells from one donor stayed in the same fold. Feature selection, preprocessing, tuning, and fitting used training donors only; no held-out donor contributed to test-to-test graph edges. The random seed was 20261031.
5. **Fit the frozen candidate and controls.** The candidate used a sigmoid gene-selection gate with L1 regularization, ridge classifier weights, smooth worst-donor cross-entropy with temperature 0.1, training-only 15-nearest-neighbor graph smoothing, and a test-time neighbor-probability blend. It used Adam with learning rate 0.001, a 500-epoch maximum, and early stopping after 25 epochs without inner-validation improvement. Ordinary L2 logistic regression was the primary comparator, with C values 0.001, 0.01, 0.1, 1, and 10. Secondary models and diagnostics were a 15-nearest-neighbor vote, linear SVM, gradient-boosted trees, three candidate ablations, cell-type-only prediction, point-size prediction, and within-cell-type checks when the frozen size rule was met. The cell-type diagnostic used only exact barcode-matched GBmap metadata; no GBmap expression values were model inputs. The point-size diagnostic used the log of QC-passed cells per point and was treated as a sampling-depth diagnostic.
6. **Score the prespecified endpoints and apply the success rule.** Class-balanced AURC was calculated per donor by integrating class-balanced risk over retained-cell coverage from `ceil(N/2)` through `N`, using the trapezoid rule over actual coverage values. Donors received equal weight in the mean. Full-coverage balanced accuracy and Brier score were secondary endpoints. The candidate could meet the frozen rule only if its mean AURC was lower than logistic regression's and it won in at least five of six donors. The candidate's mean AURC was 0.1479 versus 0.1764 for logistic regression, but it won in four donors (GS1, GS2, GS5, GS6) and lost in GS3 and GS13; the 5-of-6 rule failed, so superiority was not demonstrated. Candidate balanced accuracy was 0.6786 versus 0.6621 for logistic, while candidate Brier score was worse at 0.2335 versus 0.1944. The no-gate ablation had slightly lower AURC than the candidate (0.1473 versus 0.1479). No p-value was used to claim classifier superiority.
7. **Report diagnostics, audit execution, and state the limits.** Cell-type-only balanced accuracy was 0.6546; the best expression model on the same mapped cells reached 0.6789, a difference of 0.0243. Under the frozen rule this was comparable prediction from measured cell-type composition; it did not show that composition explained the expression result. Only the Myeloid stratum met the within-cell-type rule (at least 10 cells from each class in at least four donors), with five eligible donors; other strata did not support a cell-intrinsic claim. The linear SVM returned constant scores in all six donors. The point-size diagnostic returned constant scores in GS1, GS2, GS3, and GS5, and varied in GS6 and GS13; it was not treated as a substantive model competitor. The completed result recorded six outer folds, nine models, per-donor metrics, software versions, runtime, and source/code hashes. The execution audit confirmed the recorded source and runner hashes; the run took 10,053.63 seconds (2.793 hours). The eight-donor sensitivity analysis, calibration curve, within-library analysis, scANVI, attention analysis, and external spatial transfer were not run. A5 remains a descriptive within-cohort prediction result on mixed-cell expression and MRI-marked sampling-point labels; it does not establish malignant-cell-specific biology, causation, equivalence, clinical usefulness, or external validation. Full provenance and donor-level results are in the [frozen A5 record](Track%20A5%20Research%20Plan%20Science%20Fair%20MD%20Final.md), [execution audit](trackA_execution_audit_20261031.md), and [result JSON](../output/trackA_yu2020/result.json).
