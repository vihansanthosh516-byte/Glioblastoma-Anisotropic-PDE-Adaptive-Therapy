# Track A Research Plan — Science Fair MD Final

**Track:** A only

**Status:** A5 is complete and did not meet its frozen success rule. External confirmation is unavailable. Broad dataset scouting is paused. This plan records completed Track A work and describes a separate, conditional biology study. A5’s protocol, result, and figures stay unchanged.

## Rationale

Glioblastoma (GBM) cells and their surrounding brain environment vary across tumor core, infiltrative margin, and periphery. Infiltrating cells can remain outside the visible tumor mass. Published studies already report regional differences, infiltrating malignant-cell programs, and spatially organized tumor states. Track A first tested whether expression could predict MRI-marked Core-versus-Periphery sampling-point labels in a mixed-cell cohort. Its donor-held-out analysis did not demonstrate the frozen level of model superiority. A stronger future biology question would ask whether a regional signal remains within validated malignant cells after accounting for normal-brain content, cell composition, and established tumor-cell states. Any result would be an association, not proof of cause or treatment mechanism.

## Research Question

**Frozen A5 question:** Will a sparse, donor-balanced expression classifier improve donor-level Core-versus-Periphery prediction over ordinary logistic regression when every test donor is excluded from training?

**Separate proposed biology question:** Does a predeclared Core-versus-Periphery signal within validated malignant GBM cells transfer across at least six new, independent IDH-wildtype donors after adjustment for normal-brain content, cell-type composition, and established malignant-cell states?

The biology question does not replace or revise the frozen A5 question.

## Hypotheses

**Frozen A5 hypothesis and outcome:** The candidate was predicted to have lower mean class-balanced area under the risk-coverage curve (AURC) than ordinary L2 logistic regression and lower AURC in at least five of six primary donors. Its mean AURC was lower, 0.1479 versus 0.1764, but it won in only four of six donors. Candidate Brier score was worse, 0.2335 versus 0.1944, and the no-gate ablation had slightly lower AURC, 0.1473 versus 0.1479. The frozen success rule was not met, so superiority was not demonstrated.

**Separate proposed biology hypothesis:** If adopted after a dated amendment and all cohort gates pass, the study will test whether a predeclared regional transcriptomic signal remains detectable within defensibly labeled malignant cells across at least six new, independent IDH-wildtype GBM donors after the stated controls. The result may support, weaken, or fail to resolve the hypothesis. No malignant-cell signature is currently frozen, and no positive result is promised.

## Procedure

1. **Record the completed exploratory work and its limits.** Early work on the UCSC Cell Browser multiomic-GBM atlas reported 78.7% random-cell C-GAT accuracy, while a patient-ID lookup reached 82.8%. A later patient-held-out C-GAT run reached 63.8%, near the 64.6% cells-per-patient baseline. An exploratory expression classifier reached AUC 0.904 versus 0.713 for cell-type-only prediction, but region was confounded with sequencing library and malignant periphery cells were sparse. Three bulk regional gene-program tests also failed their prespecified adjusted success rules: SenMayo senescence/SASP had raw p = 0.0035 and hypoxia-adjusted p = 0.37; Hallmark OXPHOS had raw p = 0.0024 and p = 0.67 after hypoxia and neuron-marker adjustment; TAM activation had raw p = 0.0039 and p = 0.44 after hypoxia, neuron, and myeloid-marker adjustment. These tests do not prove the covariates explain the effects or that effects are absent. The early results are exploratory; details and limits are in the [Track A output record](../archive_old_gbm/vault/TRACK_A_RESULTS.md), [spatial handoff](trackA_spatial_handoff.md), and [composition finding](trackA_composition_finding.md).

2. **Document the completed, frozen A5 analysis without retuning it.** A5 used Yu et al. 2020 single-cell RNA-sequencing data from GEO GSE117891. Its six primary donors were GS1, GS2, GS3, GS5, GS6, and GS13. The frozen cohort rule used GBM histology and the recorded IDH1 R132 field; a dash in that field is not comprehensive proof of IDH1/2-wildtype status. Exact GEO barcode-to-sampling-point and sampling-point-to-Table S1 joins identified 75 labeled points with no unmapped target points. The analysis retained 4,139 matched tumor/peritumoral cells after quality control, including 2,519 cells from the six scored donors. Quality control required at least 1,000 UMIs and 500 detected genes. Author-supplied Tumoral points defined Core, Peritumoral points defined Periphery, and Normal points were excluded. A5 used six outer leave-one-donor-out folds and nested leave-one-training-donor-out tuning. Feature selection and preprocessing were fitted on training donors only. The candidate used a sigmoid gene-selection gate with L1 regularization, ridge classifier weights, smooth worst-donor cross-entropy loss with temperature 0.1, training-only 15-nearest-neighbor graph smoothing, and a test-time neighbor-probability blend. It used Adam at learning rate 0.001, up to 500 epochs, and early stopping after 25 epochs without inner-validation improvement. Logistic regression was the primary comparator, with C values 0.001, 0.01, 0.1, 1, and 10. Secondary controls included a 15-nearest-neighbor vote, linear SVM, gradient-boosted trees, three candidate ablations, cell-type-only prediction, point-size diagnostics, and within-cell-type checks when the frozen sample rule was met.

3. **Report A5 by donor and preserve its limits.** The candidate won in GS1, GS2, GS5, and GS6, and lost in GS3 and GS13. Mean balanced accuracy was 0.6786 for the candidate and 0.6621 for logistic regression. The cell-type-only diagnostic had balanced accuracy 0.6546; the best expression model on the same mapped cells reached 0.6789, a 0.0243 difference. Under the frozen interpretation, cell-type composition gave comparable prediction; this does not prove composition explains the expression result. Only the Myeloid stratum met the within-cell-type size rule, with five eligible donors. The linear SVM was a constant-score control in all six donors. The point-size diagnostic was a constant-score control in GS1, GS2, GS3, and GS5, and varied in GS6 and GS13. A5 is a within-cohort result on mixed-cell expression and MRI-marked point labels, not histology labels assigned to individual cells. It does not show malignant-cell-specific biology, causation, equivalence, clinical usefulness, or independent external validation. The eight-donor sensitivity cohort was not run. Calibration, within-library, scANVI, and attention analyses are **not run; deferred**; their future disposition requires an amendment. The [A5 result JSON](../output/trackA_yu2020/result.json), [execution audit](trackA_execution_audit_20261031.md), and [frozen protocol](trackA_yu2020/PROTOCOL.md) record the result and methods.

4. **Require every independent-cohort gate before any new data access.** A candidate must have public no-request access and clear dataset-specific reuse terms; at least six eligible IDH-wildtype GBM donors in the untouched validation cohort, with both regions mapped to each same donor; exact donor, sample, and region joins; per-case IDH1/2 evidence; defensible cell-level malignant labels with documented provenance and validation; exact sample and patient identifiers cross-checked against the local donor manifest, training sources, and prior-use audits; and a biologically coherent assay and region definition. Spatial spots alone do not provide malignant-cell labels. The current screen has no candidate that passes every gate. GSE286419 is the closest label-ready lead, with four retained Core/Margin pairs. GSE318564 has three explicit IDH-wildtype Core/Edge pairs and an unresolved annotation join. SNUH-GliomaAtlas has six pairs but overlaps prior project use. HRA009021 lacks a public sample-to-region map and confirmed reuse terms. Harwood Zenodo 22013127, concept record 16957940, remains unconfirmed and ineligible: donor/sample mapping, per-case IDH status, FOV-to-region mapping, annotation fields and validation, and patient-level overlap remain unresolved. Zenodo record metadata lists CC BY 4.0 for the dataset; this is separate from the article license. The paper already studies core-versus-infiltrated differences, and CosMx is a targeted spatial assay, so the lead would not establish new biology by itself. The ZIP tail requests fetched some compressed member bytes into memory; none were decompressed, inspected, or saved, and no further archive requests occurred. This was a scope deviation, not a successful listing-only read. Exact retained ranges and the missing Sample C central-directory endpoints are in the [metadata and scope-deviation plan](trackA_alternate_dataset_plan_20261101.md). Broad scouting remains paused. Reopen screening only for a specifically named accession that could plausibly pass all gates; any unknown donor map, IDH status, region label, malignant-cell validation, reuse term, or verified independence is a stop. Do not lower the six-donor minimum.

5. **Freeze the separate biology study before opening validation expression values.** If an eligible cohort is found, a dated amendment will name a discovery source and a separate untouched validation accession, the exact signature or model, the region definition, one primary donor-level endpoint, effect direction, success and precision criteria, multiplicity plan, and permitted data workflow. No analysis-ready malignant-cell signature is currently frozen. A5’s classifier is not a malignant-cell signature and will not be relabeled as one. If the study uses only a published signature, it will be framed as a transfer or replication test, not signature discovery. If no discovery source passes its gates, the project will not claim a newly discovered signature.

6. **Map samples, regions, and malignant cells with source-supported identifiers.** Before expression analysis, verify the accession, paper version, licenses, file list, donor and sample IDs, regions, per-case IDH status, cell-label method, validation evidence, and local overlap crosswalk. Join each cell to its sample, patient, region, and malignant label using documented identifiers. Count exclusions and conflicts. Apply the source’s validated cell-level malignant annotation; keep nonmalignant cells out of the malignant-cell analysis. Do not infer labels from file order, expression, suffixes, or clusters, and do not use spatial spots as malignant-cell labels. Stop if any required mapping or label evidence is unknown.

7. **Create donor-level measurements and apply the frozen controls.** Aggregate malignant-cell counts by donor and region into pseudobulk measurements, or use the alternative method stated in the amendment. Treat cells as subsamples and donors as independent units. Use matching region definitions in discovery and validation. Measure normal-brain content and cell-type composition. Compare regional expression within established malignant-cell states and standardize state composition to distinguish a state-mixture change from a within-state expression shift. Compare against published regional and cell-state signatures and expression-matched gene-set controls. Fit feature selection, scaling, models, and tuning using discovery donors only.

8. **Run untouched validation once and report the outcome accurately.** Apply the locked signature or model and normalization to validation donors without feature selection, threshold changes, or retuning. Report every donor’s paired result, the equal-donor estimate, uncertainty, and exclusions. Do not count cells or spots as independent samples. If an adjusted signal remains within malignant cells, describe a donor-level regional association; if it disappears after state standardization, report that the contrast tracked established state proportions; if it remains within states, report a residual within-state association. Any pathway or regulatory enrichment of locked residual genes will be exploratory and multiplicity-corrected. Neither a positive nor negative result establishes causation, therapy response, or clinical utility. Any external result will be presented separately; this plan does not change the current figure.

## Data Analysis

For completed A5, class-balanced AURC was the primary endpoint, averaged equally over six donors; lower AURC was better. The project calculated balanced class risk across retained-cell coverage from ceil(N/2) through N and integrated over actual coverage values with the trapezoid rule. Balanced accuracy and Brier score were secondary outcomes. The frozen candidate-success rule required both lower mean AURC and lower AURC in at least five of six primary donors; it failed. The project reported per-donor results and donor-level summaries without p-values for classifier superiority.

For the proposed study, the amendment will set one donor-level primary measure, such as the paired Core-versus-Periphery difference in a locked malignant-cell signature score from pseudobulk. It will also set the donor-level precision criterion and paired analysis before expression access. Secondary gene-level results will use a prespecified multiplicity correction. Figures may show paired donor values and a donor-level effect estimate. No cell-level p-value will be treated as independent patient evidence. Six donors are an eligibility floor, not proof of adequate power.

## Risk, Privacy, and Data Handling

Track A is computational research on previously collected human tumor data. It does not recruit people, administer surveys, use animals, culture organisms, or perform wet-lab procedures. It uses no chemicals, biological agents, or hazardous laboratory devices. The project will follow each repository’s conditions, resolve any school or institutional review required before future data use, and avoid treating research results as medical advice.
