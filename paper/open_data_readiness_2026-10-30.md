# Data-first readiness audit for the GBM research plan

Audit date: 2026-10-30. This is a decision record for the existing Tracks A, B, and C, not a replacement for the future-tense science-fair plan. The research plan is due in approximately one week; the final experiments are not necessarily due then. Newer Track A and C work is in a separate main worktree; the Track A interpretation correction is committed there as d5272ea. Do not overwrite unrelated working files.

## Decision

The user defines open data as files anyone can download and use without emailing or requesting individual permission; noncommercial-use terms may still be acceptable if they allow the intended project. Check the published terms for each exact file. A source that needs individual permission, or whose necessary reuse terms can only be resolved by obtaining permission, will not satisfy this project requirement.

No dataset has yet been established to meet every access, reuse, label, provenance, quality, sample-size, and prior-exposure condition for the currently specified Track A or Track B patient analysis. Public pages alone are not a pass. No additional large download or new patient-scoring code is justified by this audit. Track C can use generated scenarios with no external dataset, but it cannot thereby establish a new biological mechanism or treatment benefit.

## Track A: molecular and spatial study

The current aim is a donor-held-out GBM region comparison using independently defined labels. Its source manifest must establish exact expression files and their permitted use, sample-to-donor identity, sample-to-region mapping, label provenance, disease eligibility, sufficient donors with both classes, and whether any data have already been used for method or hypothesis selection. The existing plan requires at least six paired donors for descriptive analysis and at least ten before an inferential superiority claim; those are project feasibility rules, not guarantees of statistical power.

## Track B: PDE tumor-core forecasting

The current target is the source-defined tumor core from two history masks to the immediate third mask, with a nominal day-90 forecast and observed second-to-third gap of 76–104 days. Patients, not scans or voxels, are evaluation units. The primary comparator is the matched-volume no-PDE signed-distance forecast. A candidate cohort must document first-three-visit dates and masks, matching target definitions, source/reuse permissions, identity and overlap, acceptable longitudinal alignment, and valid scoring support including negative labels. The current MU loader requires source-backed, hash-bound full-grid support and registration evidence before opening target-mask arrays for scoring.

## Track C: treatment-policy simulation

Script 176 is a development run in the main worktree at cc7fd84. It simulates assumed switching and off-drug reversion; its 12-course maximum is shared across policies, but it does not prove equal actual exposure. Reversion acts during all drug-off time, including the 23 days between five-day courses, as well as during a fully skipped cycle. The filter does not measure or estimate a biological reversion rate.

The run finished on 2026-10-30. The 5,687-byte main-worktree file output/trackc_dev_plasticity/dev_results.json has SHA-256 30ac8305c455dc13f28a80faca4a54e7ec0d16eef6309be7f628eadd25909d. It reports 150 simulated scenarios and two noise replicates per suite. Selecting the strongest of the four tested holiday variants after seeing its worst-10% scores, that score was 164.5 versus 174.7 days for front-loaded dosing in the nominal suite, 132.9 versus 164.3 in assumed moderate plasticity, and 142.5 versus 267.7 in assumed strong plasticity. In that strong-plasticity suite, the same holiday rule also scored 142.5 worst-10% and 765.3 mean days versus 255.2 and 773.9 for the existing no-forced-holiday containment rule. These are descriptive development values, not a sealed test; no confidence intervals or paired per-scenario outcomes were saved. They do not support a claim that forced holidays fixed the worst-10% issue. Do not choose or retest a winner on these same scenarios as independent confirmation.

The current development script lacks a switching-without-reversion arm, uses different scenario seeds between suites, and saves aggregate outcomes rather than the paired per-scenario values needed for the proposed mechanism comparison. Therefore its result can inform development, not establish whether reversion caused a policy advantage.

Before proposing any final test, specify and freeze:

1. The candidate rule and its development-only selection procedure.
2. A no-switching/no-reversion condition, a switching-without-reversion condition, and switching-with-reversion conditions. Use matched tumor parameters and noise streams across conditions and policies.
3. Front-loaded dosing, the previous containment policy, and a strong simple periodic or holiday schedule selected with the same development budget. Give every policy the same 12-course maximum and record actual courses and timing; equal caps do not ensure equal exposure.
4. The primary mean and worst-10% outcomes, simulated scenario as the resampling unit, paired uncertainty intervals, exact suite-level decision rules, technical-failure handling, sample size, and independent test seeds. Choose sample size from a development-based precision/power and runtime assessment before opening test outcomes. Do not promise a specific runtime or statistical power without that assessment.
5. A paired difference-in-differences measuring how the policy comparison changes when reversion is added to the same switching scenarios. Keep it distinct from a simple comparison of significance across two conditions.
6. A prospectively fixed test based on the new question and documented protocol amendment, clearly described as motivated by earlier exploratory results. Preserve and disclose the script 171 and 173 H1 failures and their existing −7-day simulation margin; a different experiment is a new test, not a rewrite of those results. Do not change the old margin or claim that a new experiment passed the old test.

**Biology and reuse check:** [Segura-Collar et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9616068/) already studied GBM treatment spacing and reversible phenotypes. Adding breaks or reversion alone is not a supported novelty claim. A narrower contribution might be a transparent robustness comparison under uncertainty, subject to further prior-art review. State that simulated reversion rates are assumptions.

[Dryad 10.5061/dryad.905qfttx0](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.905qfttx0) lists a 60.91 KB workbook of source values for GBM experiments, and [Dryad terms](https://datadryad.org/terms) state publication under CC0. Its study concerns EGFR-inhibitor timing and temozolomide, not the current reversion mechanism. The attempted web download returned HTTP 403; actual file access, sheets, and suitability are unverified. It must not be described as a cleared validation dataset for this policy.

[Joun et al. 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-65888-5) provides a closer biological lead: its methods describe drug-tolerant GBM cell lines recovering in drug-free media, followed by experimental measurements; its article reports an available source-data workbook and public sequencing series [GSE279066](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE279066). The paper is CC BY 4.0, but the source workbook's exact downloadable bytes, relevant sheets, per-file terms, and suitability remain to be checked. The experiment uses different drugs and cell models; it does not establish a temozolomide-specific human reversion rate or validate this policy.

A separate [temozolomide cell-culture study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4825557/) reported partial recovery of drug sensitivity in two GBM-derived cultures after at least 30 days without treatment. This single reported recovery time is biological context, not a measured recovery curve or a human transition rate. Machine-readable source data and file-specific reuse terms are not yet verified.

## Before any new implementation

For each proposed analysis, complete a source table naming: release and direct file URLs; exact license and allowed processing; relevant fields and labels; independent evaluation unit and available counts; provenance and prior exposure; quality checks; primary target and comparator; compute and runtime; failure and contingency rules. Record PASS, FAIL, or UNKNOWN for every required item with a supporting source or actual metadata inspection. Begin new analysis code only for an analysis whose essential inputs and terms pass. Document any remaining uncertainty in the submitted research plan and follow the fair's prior-work disclosure requirements.
