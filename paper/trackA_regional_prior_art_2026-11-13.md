# Track A regional expression methods search

Scope: predict which of two independently annotated specimens from a new IDH-wildtype glioblastoma donor is the infiltrative margin, using expression in malignant cells. Screen adjacent studies for portable scores and confound controls. Survival, cultured drug sensitivity, recurrence, migration assays, and imaging-only models answer different endpoints.

## Search record

Searched web-indexed PubMed, PMC, publisher Methods sections, GEO sample and series descriptions, and existing Track A source inventories. Query families combined glioblastoma with core/periphery, core/edge, margin, infiltrative, regional classifier, signature, malignant single-cell, pseudobulk, spatial, patient-held-out, matched gene ranks, and top-scoring pairs. Followed Pai's cross-signature comparisons back to their sources. Checked Petry by author, title, DOI and GEO terms and checked the recent multi-sampling paper's data-availability lead. Publisher access failed for those two papers, so their full methods and supplements remain unchecked. This is a scoped methods inventory with identified gaps. It cannot establish exhaustive coverage or priority.

| Method family or score | Published precedent and benchmark implication |
|---|---|
| Histology-linked expression markers and atlas centroids | [Ivy GAP](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6414061/) links microdissected regions to expression. Use a frozen leading-edge versus cellular-tumor marker or centroid score, with explicit mixed-tissue provenance. [Matrisome study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12769532/) motivates a separate extracellular matrix module score. |
| Infiltration DEG panels | [Darmanis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5810554/) inferred neoplastic cells and derived an infiltration signature. [Pai](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12603948/) used single-cell and pseudobulk differential expression, published margin markers including EGFR, and compared external signatures. Benchmark each available fixed panel, EGFR alone, and a training-only paired DEG score. |
| Pathway and spatial-layer programs | [Harwood](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-52167-y) used targeted spatial profiling, NMF and infiltration/Notch/synaptic programs. [Greenwald](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11088502/) identified hypoxia-linked layers. Benchmark published invasion and layer scores where fixed weights or sets are retrievable. Include hypoxia and known malignant-state scores. |
| Region/niche mapping from mixtures | [Sonpatki](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-69716-2) combines histology, composition, and gene signatures. [Petti](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.04.04.647096v3.full) uses spatial and expression niche models. [de Jong](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11234-8) maps nuclei to Visium with cell2location and models malignant-state zonation and trajectories. Compare the portable expression/state summaries. Full spatial pipelines require coordinates and tissue context and cannot be reproduced on expression-only inputs. Expression-derived labels cannot serve as independent ground truth. |
| Malignant-state adjustment | [GBM longitudinal atlas](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12081298/) already balances malignant cell states across paired specimens by downsampling. [Pai](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12603948/) compares Neftel modules by region. State balancing alone has precedent. Compare state proportions, balanced and ordinary pseudobulk, and nuisance-only scores. |
| Other regional programs | [Petry](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42844997/) reports iron-associated invasive states. [Multi-sampling study](https://academic.oup.com/neuro-oncology/article/28/9/2136/8708331) compares enhancement-linked RNA profiles and subtype/pathway summaries. Screen available fixed definitions before freezing their benchmark adapters. |
| Standard classifiers | Recover logistic regression, gradient boosting and other published Track A arms from the [A5 protocol](trackA_yu2020/PROTOCOL.md). Benchmark paired regularized logistic regression, nearest centroid and nonlinear classifiers. A5's original cell-level score is a different endpoint. |
| Relative expression order | [TSP](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1989150/), [generalized TSP](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/27/12/1729/256967), and [MetaKTSP](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/32/13/1966/1743805) establish gene-order classifiers and cross-study aggregation. Compare TSP, k-TSP and MetaKTSP when multiple development studies qualify. Relative ranks and cross-study stability alone have precedent. |

## Fresh-cohort status

The [alternate-cohort audit](trackA_alternate_dataset_plan_20261101.md) covers access, pairs, exact region mapping and independence. Yu, SNUH and Ivy have prior local exposure. Petti's single-cell deposit and Pai's pathology-confirmed subset fall short of the existing donor floor. HGCC is spent and addresses measured drug response. Petry is a lead pending a usable accession and provenance. The multi-sampling study needs a verified expression manifest and malignant-cell resolution. [GSE275293](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE275293) describes a patient myeloid expression file, which cannot serve as malignant-cell data. No fresh test cohort was verified. No candidate test expression or assay matrix was accessed.
