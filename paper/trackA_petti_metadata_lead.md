# Track A metadata lead: Petti lab spatial GBM

[GSE335275 BioProject](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/PRJNA1477838) and [example GSM](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM9809294) identify spatial files including cell coordinates and segmentation. A repeated patient code appears at [GSM9809293](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM9809293) and [GSM9809294](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM9809294). Repeated samples alone do not establish a core versus infiltrating pair. The related [series](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE318562) reports assay coverage, not eligible pair counts.

Status: lead only. Require a source-backed mapping from specimen to patient and independent pathology region, adequate paired patients, and an overlap check. No expression or outcomes were inspected in this screen.

The [study's methods](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.04.04.647096v3.full) document manual histology labels for high-density core and low-density infiltrating edge. The [Zenodo deposit](https://zenodo.org/records/15014838) includes annotated Xenium objects. Audit whether the histology masks can be registered to the Xenium cells and deduplicate patients across Visium and Xenium. Include density-only and composition-only baselines because density partly defines the study labels. Eligibility remains open.
