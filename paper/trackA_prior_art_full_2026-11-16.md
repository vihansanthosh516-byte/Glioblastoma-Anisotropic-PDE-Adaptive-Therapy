# Track A full scoped prior-art review

Search date: 2026-11-16  
Scope: pathology-defined GBM core versus infiltrative edge/margin biology, plus direct glioma drug-response methods relevant to the earlier TMZ route.

## Decision

The regional biology is not new, and the main computational ingredients already have substantial precedents. A distinct, testable candidate is defined below: a maximin patient-paired gene-order classifier. The search did not establish that this exact optimization objective has never been used. **Novelty is unresolved; do not describe the candidate as a unique method or a new biological discovery before the benchmark.**

This search is an operational inventory of primary GBM regional studies, directly related classifier and spatial-method families, and direct glioma response methods. “All methods” cannot mean every generic algorithm that could be applied to expression data. The inventory records all direct and adjacent methods identified in the stated scope and marks input or endpoint mismatches instead of pretending they are executable comparators.

## Search scope and failure context

Searches covered PubMed/PMC, publisher pages, primary-paper supplements where available, and software repositories. Query families included: GBM core/periphery/infiltrating margin paired spatial transcriptomics; pathology-guided region prediction with patient-held-out validation; gene-pair/TSP relative-order classifiers; paired and stable feature selection; maximin/worst-subject classifier selection; spatial-neighborhood correction and spatial differential expression; and measured TMZ response in patient-derived glioma cultures. The review includes sources returned by searches and cross-references in relevant primary studies. It is not a systematic review of every cancer, every imaging-only method, or every generic classifier.

Track A’s earlier A5 model did not pass its frozen donor-consistency rule: the candidate won four of six donors against logistic regression, below the rule of five of six. The model’s average class-balanced AURC did not rescue that failure. Composition-only performance was close to the strongest expression model, while the within-myeloid check was near chance; these controls suggest that cell mixture may contribute, but do not establish causation. See the [failure audit](trackA_failure_audit_2026-11-13.md), [frozen A5 result](../output/trackA_yu2020/result.json), and [A5 protocol](trackA_yu2020/PROTOCOL.md).

The TMZ results test different endpoints and both failed to support a response claim. The regional Ntafoulis-panel proxy had split donor directions and an exact sign-flip result recorded in the [regional result and amendment](trackA_tmz_response_119of121_amendment_2026-11-08.md) and [saved output](../output/trackA_tmz_response_proxy_119of121/result.json). The later HGCC bridge compared a fixed 102-gene score with measured in-vitro viability AUC and reported no association; the [result](../output/trackA_hgcc_tmz_102/result.json) and [interpretation](trackA_hgcc_tmz_102_result_2026-11-12.md) identify platform, missing-gene, and culture-transfer limits without claiming which caused the null. ## Existing region-classification and expression-method families

### Relative gene-order classifiers

These are the closest algorithmic prior art to the candidate below. They use within-sample gene-order indicators, so they already provide scale-robust pair features.

|---|---|---|

### Matched-pair selection and standard predictors

Paired t-tests and fold-change rankings, conditional logistic regression, penalized conditional logistic regression, random-penalized CLR, PCU-CLR, Bayesian variable-selection CLR, WL2Boost, PQLBoost, paired SVM, Gaussian naive Bayes, and logistic regression have all been used for matched expression studies. Stable-CLR and patient-resampled stability selection are direct precedents for robust feature selection. Generic expression-classifier families include ridge/LASSO/elastic-net logistic regression, linear or kernel SVM, naive Bayes, random forests, boosting, nearest-centroid, and paired-difference classifiers. These are not individually novel; fair evaluation must tune and select features inside patient-grouped training folds. [Matched-method review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6158772/); [penalized CLR](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11212437/); [stability selection](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9868.2010.00740.x).

## One candidate method and exact novelty boundary

### Candidate: maximin patient-paired rank-pair vote (MP-RPV)

For each gene pair and each training patient, compute a within-specimen order vote `v = sign(x_g1 - x_g2)` in the core and margin pseudobulk. The patient’s paired contrast is `d_i = v_margin - v_core`, with expression ties represented by zero. For each pair, choose the orientation `s` in `{-1,+1}` that maximizes `min_i(s*d_i)` across training patients. Rank pairs by that worst-patient contrast, break ties by larger mean oriented contrast and then gene-symbol order, and take the first five strictly positive pairs. Give selected pairs equal vote weight. Score the two specimens from a held-out patient and call margin only if its score is higher. If no positive pair remains, issue a tie; do not back off to a weaker selector.

This is intended as a **narrow algorithmic variant**: choose relative-expression pairs by worst-training-patient signed contrast, rather than by class ordering-proportion difference, AUC, aggregate vote, a mean effect, random-effects replication, or selection frequency. TSP/k-TSP, AUCTSP, MetaKTSP, the pGLMM pair method, and the GBM rank-pair ensemble are direct prior art on representation or patient/study consistency. The local search did not find the exact same maximin objective, but that is not proof of uniqueness; generic robust optimization and a wider source-code/reference search remain. The previous weakest-state pair proposal is a different candidate and failed its prespecified simulation; its result must not be hidden or replaced as if it had succeeded. [Candidate simulation result](trackA_margin_simulation_result_2026-11-13.md); [saved simulation JSON](../output/trackA_margin_simulation/result.json); [closest rank-pair methods](#relative-gene-order-classifiers).

### Prospective benchmark set

The candidate is benchmarked only for the region-label task, on the same patient-held-out predictions and paired-concordance score. Methods that require region labels to estimate a differential effect, extra modalities absent from the assay, multiple independent development studies, or a drug-response endpoint are listed above but are not misrepresented as a deployable classifier. The frozen protocol must list exact portable adapters, software/version/source, feature definitions, patient-level splits and exclusion rules before any Artzi expression matrix is opened.

Nearest predictor comparators to freeze are TSP, k-TSP, AUCTSP, the published GBM rank-pair/Robencla procedure if reproducible in a single cohort, the penalized pair-order GLMM if it can be fit and predict a new patient without test information, paired centroid, regularized paired logistic regression, linear SVM, a tree/boosting classifier, a training-only paired differential-expression score, and every portable published GBM region signature with a published gene set and direction. Fixed signatures from studies whose patient overlap with Artzi cannot be excluded must be labeled exploratory. Direct predictors not frozen, or absent signature definitions, block the primary benchmark; they cannot quietly disappear after results are seen.

The only common primary score is mean patient-level paired concordance: for one held-out patient, a win is `score_margin > score_core`, a loss is the reverse, and a tie contributes one-half. Each patient contributes once; cells and fields of view never count as independent replicates. The prospective protocol must freeze the minimum eligible-patient count and test rule based on the complete comparator count. Do not use cell-level accuracy, region-level random splitting, or different metrics for different methods.

## Limits and next decision

The method can be benchmarked as an exploratory patient-held-out analysis if the required matrix-to-metadata join is valid. Artzi has at most ten metadata-defined primary core/infiltrative-edge patient pairs; the exact same-resection link is inferred because a specimen-level surgery ID is absent. These are documented in the [metadata-only audit](trackA_artzi_zenodo_metadata_2026-11-16.md). The study’s pooled expression-derived malignancy and state labels will not be treated as independent features. A whole-tissue pseudobulk prediction concerns the complete regional ecosystem, not tumor-cell-intrinsic biology.

The cohort was chosen after prior negative Track A results, so even a frozen patient-held-out benchmark is exploratory and cannot establish independent external validation or clinical utility. Yu and HGCC stay spent. Do not open the Artzi expression matrix until the separate dated pre-analysis plan and complete comparator manifest have been committed.
