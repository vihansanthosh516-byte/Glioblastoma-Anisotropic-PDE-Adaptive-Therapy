# Track A full scoped prior-art review

Search date: 2026-11-16  
Scope: pathology-defined GBM core versus infiltrative edge/margin biology, plus direct glioma drug-response methods relevant to the earlier TMZ route.

## Decision

The regional biology is not new, and the main computational ingredients already have substantial precedents. A distinct, testable candidate is defined below: a maximin patient-paired gene-order classifier. The search did not establish that this exact optimization objective has never been used. **Novelty is unresolved; do not describe the candidate as a unique method or a new biological discovery before the benchmark.**

This search is an operational inventory of primary GBM regional studies, directly related classifier and spatial-method families, and direct glioma response methods. “All methods” cannot mean every generic algorithm that could be applied to expression data. The inventory records all direct and adjacent methods identified in the stated scope and marks input or endpoint mismatches instead of pretending they are executable comparators.

## Search scope and failure context

Searches covered PubMed/PMC, publisher pages, primary-paper supplements where available, and software repositories. Query families included: GBM core/periphery/infiltrating margin paired spatial transcriptomics; pathology-guided region prediction with patient-held-out validation; gene-pair/TSP and monotone bivariate classifiers; paired and stable feature selection; maximin/worst-subject classifier selection; spatial-neighborhood correction and spatial differential expression; and measured TMZ response in patient-derived glioma cultures. The review includes sources returned by searches and cross-references in relevant primary studies. It is not a systematic review of every cancer, every imaging-only method, or every generic classifier.

Track A’s earlier A5 model did not pass its frozen donor-consistency rule: the candidate won four of six donors against logistic regression, below the rule of five of six. The model’s average class-balanced AURC did not rescue that failure. Composition-only performance was close to the strongest expression model, while the within-myeloid check was near chance; these controls suggest that cell mixture may contribute, but do not establish causation. See the [failure audit](trackA_failure_audit_2026-11-13.md), [frozen A5 result](../output/trackA_yu2020/result.json), and [A5 protocol](trackA_yu2020/PROTOCOL.md).

The TMZ results test different endpoints and both failed to support a response claim. The regional Ntafoulis-panel proxy had split donor directions and an exact sign-flip result recorded in the [regional result and amendment](trackA_tmz_response_119of121_amendment_2026-11-08.md) and [saved output](../output/trackA_tmz_response_proxy_119of121/result.json). The later HGCC bridge compared a fixed 102-gene score with measured in-vitro viability AUC and reported no association; the [result](../output/trackA_hgcc_tmz_102/result.json) and [interpretation](trackA_hgcc_tmz_102_result_2026-11-12.md) identify platform, missing-gene, and culture-transfer limits without claiming which caused the null. Yu and HGCC are spent for test use and will not be reopened.

## Existing region-classification and expression-method families

### Relative gene-order classifiers

These are the closest algorithmic prior art to the candidate below. They use within-sample gene-order indicators, so they already provide scale-robust pair features.

| Method | Established method | Source |
|---|---|---|
| TSP | Selects a gene pair whose within-sample ordering frequency differs between classes. | [Geman et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1989150/); [tspair](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btp126) |
| k-TSP / switchBox | Combines multiple gene pairs using a vote; direct baseline for a multi-pair rank classifier. | [Tan et al.](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/21/20/3896/203010); [switchBox](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4287945/) |
| Generalized TSP / Rgtsp | Extends top-scoring pair rules to multiclass classification. | [Lin et al.](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/27/12/1729/256967); [Rgtsp](https://github.com/bioinfo-recetox/Rgtsp) |
| AUCTSP | Chooses a pair using an AUC-based objective. | [Primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6020231/); [code](https://github.com/SIU852343578/AUC-TSP) |
| MetaKTSP | Aggregates k-TSP information across independent studies for cross-study prediction. Its intended multi-study setting is not available if only one dataset is used. | [Primary paper](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/32/13/1966/1743805); [code](https://github.com/metaOmic/MetaKTSP) |
| Top-scoring N / triplets | Generalizes relative-order predictors beyond pairs. | [Primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3663421/) |
| BMC / ensembleBMC / MBMC | Learns monotone decision surfaces from two-gene expression pairs; the recent MBMC paper applies ordinal pair ensembles to GBM survival labels. This is close algorithmic prior art for gene-pair classifiers. Its reported outcome is survival, not pathology region; the benchmark adapts the public binary implementation to the paired region endpoint. | [MBMC paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13238737/); [source code](https://github.com/oceanefrqt/MBMC); [binary BMC source](https://doi.org/10.1093/infdis/jiy086) |
| Pair t-score | Projects each gene pair onto a diagonal linear-discriminant axis, ranks pairs by class separation, and can select disjoint pairs for downstream prediction. | [Method review and specification](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s43556-025-00263-w) |
| Gene expression ratios (GER) | Classifies using gene-expression ratios and a threshold at one; can combine multiple ratios by voting. | [Method review and specification](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s43556-025-00263-w) |
| Pairwise SVM ensemble (PSVM) | Repeatedly trains SVMs on gene pairs and retains pairs passing a repeated validation threshold before majority voting. | [Shi et al.](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2105-12-375); [method review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s43556-025-00263-w) |
| Top-scoring pair of groups (TSPG) | Repeatedly samples training data, selects disjoint TSP pairs, and retains stable genes for a rank-group classifier. | [Method review and specification](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s43556-025-00263-w) |
| k-TSP plus SVM | Feeds selected k-TSP pair indicators to an SVM rather than using only the original majority vote. | [Shi et al.](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2105-12-375); [method review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s43556-025-00263-w) |
| REO plus machine learning | Selects class-reversing relative-expression orderings, ranks the pair features, then trains a classifier such as SVM. | [Zhang et al.](https://doi.org/10.3389/fbioe.2020.00254); [method review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s43556-025-00263-w) |
| TSP plus machine learning | Combines TSP feature generation with feature ranking and classifier selection. The reviewed procedure performs a data-dependent feature and model search; the benchmark freezes one transparent SVM adapter. | [Method review and proposed pipeline](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s43556-025-00263-w) |
| Rank-pair trees/ensembles | Builds tree or ensemble predictors from pair-order indicators. | [Primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10848444/); [code](https://github.com/TransBioInfoLab/ranktreeEnsemble) |
| Penalized GLMM pair method | Encodes within-sample gene-pair order as predictors and fits fixed plus study-level random effects to select effects reproducible across studies. This is close prior art for robust pair selection, but a single Artzi cohort cannot estimate the source method’s between-study variation. | [Rashid et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7528965/); [implementation article](https://journal.r-project.org/articles/RJ-2023-034/) |
| Constrained gene-ratio regression | Fits a sparse model on log gene ratios with a sum-to-zero coefficient constraint, reducing the full pairwise-ratio model to gene-level coefficients. It is another direct relative-expression modeling precedent; the benchmark uses a binary logistic adapter. | [Primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5670721/) |
| GBM rank-pair robust ensemble | Predicts GBM sex-associated clusters across expression datasets using gene-pair ordering, class ordering proportions, and cross-dataset concordance. It targets cluster labels rather than pathology regions but is close disease-specific prior art. | [Primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11815886/); [study code](https://github.com/Gibbsdavidl/GBM_Sex_Specific_Cluster_Prediction); [Robencla](https://github.com/gibbsdavidl/robencla) |
| RankComp family | Uses stable relative orderings and reversals to identify differential genes; adjacent because it is not a direct region classifier. | [RankComp](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btu522); [REOA](https://github.com/pathint/reoa); [RankCompV3](https://github.com/pathint/RankCompV3.jl) |
| TimiGP | Uses transcriptomic gene-pair features to characterize the tumor immune microenvironment. Its cell-composition/immune endpoint does not define a pathology core-versus-edge classifier. | [Gene-pair methods review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s43556-025-00263-w) |
| RMCT and EMTTree+RX | Decision-tree methods built from relative-expression features in multi-omics classification settings. The source task and data inputs differ from this single-assay regional endpoint. | [Gene-pair methods review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s43556-025-00263-w) |

### Matched-pair selection and standard predictors

Paired t-tests and fold-change rankings, conditional logistic regression, penalized conditional logistic regression, random-penalized CLR, PCU-CLR, Bayesian variable-selection CLR, WL2Boost, PQLBoost, paired SVM, Gaussian naive Bayes, and logistic regression have all been used for matched expression studies. Stable-CLR and patient-resampled stability selection are direct precedents for robust feature selection. Generic expression-classifier families include ridge/LASSO/elastic-net logistic regression, linear or kernel SVM, naive Bayes, random forests, boosting, decision trees, Gaussian processes, nearest-centroid, and paired-difference classifiers. The MBMC study itself compares logistic regression, linear/RBF SVM, random forest, decision tree, and Gaussian process classifiers on GBM survival labels; the benchmark includes those standard model families with fixed adapters. These methods are not individually novel; fair evaluation must tune and select features inside patient-grouped training folds. [Matched-method review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6158772/); [penalized CLR](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11212437/); [stability selection](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9868.2010.00740.x); [MBMC study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13238737/).

## Existing direct glioma drug-response methods

These methods establish prior art for the earlier drug-response branch, but they cannot be scored against pathology-region prediction. The Artzi study reports treatment history, not measured drug response, so it does not provide a response endpoint for a new TMZ benchmark.

| Skaga et al. | Dose-response in patient-specific GSC cultures and drug-sensitivity summaries related to survival and MGMT. | [Primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9483808/) |

## One candidate method and exact novelty boundary

### Candidate: maximin patient-paired rank-pair vote (MP-RPV)

For each gene pair and each training patient, compute a within-specimen order vote `v = sign(x_g1 - x_g2)` in the core and margin pseudobulk. The patient’s paired contrast is `d_i = v_margin - v_core`, with expression ties represented by zero. For each pair, choose the orientation `s` in `{-1,+1}` that maximizes `min_i(s*d_i)` across training patients. Rank pairs by that worst-patient contrast, break ties by larger mean oriented contrast and then gene-symbol order, and take the first five strictly positive pairs. Give selected pairs equal vote weight. Score the two specimens from a held-out patient and call margin only if its score is higher. If no positive pair remains, issue a tie; do not back off to a weaker selector.

This is intended as a **narrow algorithmic variant**: choose relative-expression pairs by worst-training-patient signed contrast, rather than by class ordering-proportion difference, AUC, aggregate vote, a mean effect, random-effects replication, monotone boundary fitting, or selection frequency. TSP/k-TSP, AUCTSP, MetaKTSP, BMC/MBMC, the pGLMM pair method, and the GBM rank-pair ensemble are direct prior art on representation or patient/study consistency. The scoped search did not find this exact patient-paired maximin objective. Generic maximin robust rank tests and worst-subgroup model optimization already exist, so novelty remains narrow and unresolved; this is not proof of global uniqueness. The previous weakest-state pair proposal is a different candidate and failed its prespecified simulation; its result must not be hidden or replaced as if it had succeeded. [Candidate simulation result](trackA_margin_simulation_result_2026-11-13.md); [saved simulation JSON](../output/trackA_margin_simulation/result.json); [closest rank-pair methods](#relative-gene-order-classifiers); [maximin robust rank-test example](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22173706/); [worst-subgroup model comparison](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.12250).

### Prospective benchmark set

The candidate is benchmarked only for the region-label task, on the same patient-held-out predictions and paired-concordance score. Methods that require region labels to estimate a differential effect, extra modalities absent from the assay, multiple independent development studies, or a drug-response endpoint are listed above but are not misrepresented as a deployable classifier. The amended [pre-analysis plan](trackA_artzi_preanalysis_2026-11-16.md) freezes portable adapters, source/versions, feature definitions, patient-level splits, comparator arms, and exclusion rules before Artzi expression access.

The amended plan freezes TSP, k-TSP, AUCTSP, top-scoring triplets, rank-pair trees, BMC/MBMC, the Kersch classifier adapter, constrained gene-ratio regression, standard paired/logistic/SVM/tree baselines, train-only paired differential expression, and source-defined signatures from Bastola, 5-ALA, and Saadawy. It records other regional/spatial methods as structurally ineligible when their output or inputs cannot be transferred to the specified patient-held-out pseudobulk score. Fixed signatures from studies whose patient overlap with Artzi cannot be excluded are exploratory. The full arm list and exact rules are in the [pre-analysis plan](trackA_artzi_preanalysis_2026-11-16.md).

The only common primary score is mean patient-level paired concordance: for one held-out patient, a win is `score_margin > score_core`, a loss is the reverse, and a tie contributes one-half. Each patient contributes once; cells and fields of view never count as independent replicates. The prospective protocol must freeze the minimum eligible-patient count and test rule based on the complete comparator count. Do not use cell-level accuracy, region-level random splitting, or different metrics for different methods.

## Limits and next decision

The method can be benchmarked as an exploratory patient-held-out analysis if the required matrix-to-metadata join is valid. Artzi has at most ten metadata-defined primary core/infiltrative-edge patient pairs; the exact same-resection link is inferred because a specimen-level surgery ID is absent. These are documented in the [metadata-only audit](trackA_artzi_zenodo_metadata_2026-11-16.md). The study’s pooled expression-derived malignancy and state labels will not be treated as independent features. A whole-tissue pseudobulk prediction concerns the complete regional ecosystem, not tumor-cell-intrinsic biology.

The cohort was chosen after prior negative Track A results, so even a frozen patient-held-out benchmark is exploratory and cannot establish independent external validation or clinical utility. Yu and HGCC stay spent. The Artzi expression matrix remains sealed until the amended dated pre-analysis plan, complete comparator manifest, and analysis code have been committed.
