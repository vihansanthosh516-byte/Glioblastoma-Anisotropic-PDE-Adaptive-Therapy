# Track A outlier block simulation plan

Simulation follow-up to [adaptive weighting](trackA_adaptive_result.md). Reuse the generator, sample sizes and replicate count from the [order reversal plan](trackA_order_simulation_plan.md) and its [base plan](trackA_block_simulation_plan_2026-11-17.md), with new seed 20261121. Use diffuse reversal and null, each clean and contaminated. Contamination replaces one independently and uniformly selected block per patient-region with depths allocated by a multinomial draw. Its probabilities are the original block's empirical proportions multiplied by exp(z), then renormalized, where z is independently Normal(0, 3) per gene. Apply the same contamination mechanism to training and test.

Candidate: paired logistic regression on region features formed by concatenating median block log expression and mean block gene-order votes. Use the existing log transform and fixed adjacent pairs. Use C=1 and zero intercept with paired contrast and its negative. Compare with median-only logistic, mean block logistic, pooled logistic, and soft, hard and pooled order votes from the base plan. All methods use the same draws and paired concordance. Compare median-only logistic directly to isolate the contribution of order votes.

Pass only if candidate beats every comparator by at least 0.01 in contaminated reversal, trails the best comparator by no more than 0.02 in clean reversal, and has mean null concordance within 0.02 of 0.50 under both null conditions. Simulation only. Fixed feature combination has no established novelty. No spent cohort access.
