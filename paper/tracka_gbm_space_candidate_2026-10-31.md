# Track A public-data candidate: GBM-Space

Status: promising source under feasibility review; not a scoring authorization or independent confirmation. This record preserves source evidence and unknowns without changing the current spatial research question.

The [GBM-Space portal](https://www.gbmspace.org/) describes 12 tumors and 97 Visium sections with histopathology information. The current [E-MTAB-17164 study record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/arrayexpress/studies/E-MTAB-17164) lists individual publicly accessible per-section H5AD files and Space Ranger data including H&E images. The source's [H5AD README](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/files/E-MTAB-17164/README_visium_anndata.md) describes spot identifiers, section/sample identifiers, tissue coordinates, H&E images, gene counts and, for certain sections, histopathology annotation overlap as distinct feature types in the same matrix. Retain gene expression separately from label features; never feed a label or its expression-derived proxy to the model as a predictor.

The public SDRF contains 97 data rows, 97 unique sample and assay identifiers, and 12 distinct individual identifiers with glioblastoma recorded. The 98 physical text lines include the header; this reconciles with the source record's 97 assays and 97 listed H5AD files. The source must also establish IDH status, whether enough distinct patients have independently defined core and edge regions in the relevant sections, and how the pathology labels were produced. Having 12 patient identifiers does not establish 10 paired donors or suitable labels.

Using public HTTP byte-range access, the audit inspected HDF5 feature metadata for `AT13-BRA-3-FO-1_1.h5ad`; no full H5AD, expression matrix, label values or H&E image was downloaded for this check. Its feature-type categories include histopath annotation overlap. Two feature names in that category are `Leading edge (grey matter)` and `Leading edge (white matter)`; no exact core feature was listed for that section. A second metadata inspection of `AT3-BRA5-FO-1_2.h5ad` found `Infiltrating tumor (grey matter)` and `Leading edge (grey matter)` overlap features. These feature names alone do not verify the label values, their independence from expression, or cohort-wide coverage. A transfer attempt for the second file produced fewer bytes than the reported full file size; it is not a verified complete download. Per-file metadata, preprocessing and analysis exposure remain to be audited before any test. The authors request citation to De Jong et al. in their README.

Additional metadata checks found five pathology feature names in `AT10-BRA-5-FO-2_1.h5ad`, including `Cellular tumor`, whereas `AT11-BRA-6-FO-1_1.h5ad` had no pathology feature category. These checks support section-specific label availability, not the existence of paired core/edge labels within those sections or patients. Counts and annotation values remain unknown.

Two further sections, `AT15-BRA-4-FO-A2-S21.h5ad` and `AT3-BRA5-FO-1_1.h5ad`, list both `Cellular tumor` and infiltrating-tumor feature names. This is a potentially useful same-section feature-name combination, but feature names alone do not establish nonzero annotated spots, whether infiltrating tumor meets the locked edge definition, label independence, or enough eligible donors. `AT14-BRA-4-FO-2_1.h5ad` had no pathology feature category. No expression matrix or annotation values were opened for these checks.

The [BioStudies submission policy](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/submit) says new BioStudies datasets are released under CC0; the record's own explicit file-level license was not independently located in this audit. Verify the applicability of the stated repository policy and any file-specific conditions to the exact intended files rather than treating policy or public download alone as a complete reuse audit. No individual request for access is needed for the public file and metadata reads performed here.

Next: verify individual-to-donor identities and inventory the required histopathology features in the section files without reading expression or labels, verify source annotation provenance and exact terms, and establish whether the existing same-section donor thresholds are actually met. Keep the current study blocked until the relevant gates pass. A revised question would need a documented amendment, not an assumed substitution.
