## 2. Project shape

- **Track A — molecular and spatial data:** single-cell and spatial GBM analyses, including cell-state or tumor-zone prediction.
- **Track B — tumor growth:** longitudinal MRI, reaction-diffusion/PDE models, parameter estimation, and forecasting.
- **Track C — treatment suppression:** adaptive therapy, optimal control, and reinforcement-learning simulations.

These tracks share a GBM focus, but use different cohorts and endpoints. The repository does not establish a patient-level join from Track A to the MRI cohorts. Do not describe the existing work as one fused patient-level model or as a validated digital twin.

## 3. What the old evidence says

- **Track A:** the random-cell split leaked patient identity. The recorded patient-level C-GAT accuracy is 0.638, close to the cells-per-patient baseline of 0.646. Donor composition remains a confound.
- **Track B:** existing PDE forecasts did not demonstrate an advantage over simple baselines. The fitted diffusion parameter hit a bound in most cases in the recorded inverse problem.
- **Track C:** treatment suppression results are simulations using assumed drug-effect values. The “real_test” policy result uses fitted growth rates but assumed kill rates; it is not a real-drug evaluation.
- **Cross-track link:** the recorded Track A-to-B score was constant, and the source lineage is unresolved. No molecular-to-MRI patient linkage is established.

- **Track B synthetic parameter recovery (2026-10-22):** new `src/fk_mask_inverse.py` fits isotropic Fisher-Kolmogorov (rho, D) to a series of binary masks with the TensorFK solver. `tests/test_fk_parameter_recovery.py` makes reference masks at 1 mm, block-averages them to 2 mm, and fits at 2 mm (ellipsoid octant, scans at days 0/60/120/180). With three follow-ups the Jacobian has full rank, and equal-front-speed pairs give different scans, so rho and D were separately identifiable in this setup. With one follow-up a ridge remains: only a combination is constrained. Fits are off the bounds, at least as good as the truth, Dice >= 0.95. Log errors (rho, D): 0.03, 0.066 at truth (0.03, 0.1); 0.162, 0.408 at truth (0.05, 0.03), where D is about 33% low. Both stay within the linearised bias from the measured 1 mm vs 2 mm mismatch (up to 5% log volume at the truth). Weakest point: 2 mm discretisation alone biases D by up to about 1.5x in the second case. The script-51 volume surrogate puts D on its lower bound and misses rho by more than 2x on the same PDE volumes (kept as a limitation test). Scratch-clone suite 172 passed (the recovery test takes about 2 minutes). No patient scans fitted; this is not evidence of recovery from real scans.
- **Track B preliminary synthetic screen (2026-10-24):** `src/fk_synthetic_scenario_grid.py` compares PDE-only, persistence, volume-trend, and the hybrid that takes PDE shape with volume-trend size. Calibration is separate by generating condition (growth truth × assumed boundary-noise level); held-out masks are scoring-only. The 64-case synthetic screen used 32 calibration and 32 evaluation cases, 8 per split in each of four conditions, with four bootstrap refits per case. On the 32 evaluation cases, mean Dice was 0.618 for PDE-only, 0.781 for persistence, 0.836 for volume-trend, and 0.873 for the hybrid; the hybrid had higher Dice than volume-trend in 26/32 cases while their volume errors were identical by construction. Calibration coverage varied by score and condition (50% to 100% across cells at an 80% target), with some bounds reaching the uninformative ceiling of 1.0. - **Track B spatial uncertainty and score utilities (2026-10-24):** new `src/fk_spatial_uncertainty.py` selects deterministic surface anchors, samples the exponential covariance kernel, interpolates three-nearest-anchor displacements into a signed-distance boundary band, and calculates spatial variogram, marginal Brier, energy, and volume CRPS scores. Five scratch tests check the kernel, reproducibility, boundary-pair design, and hand-calculated scores. - **Track B volume-model utilities (2026-10-24):** new `src/fk_volume_model.py` supports negative log-volume slopes, shrinks short-gap patient slopes toward a supplied training-population slope, selects the time constant from caller-supplied training validation cases, fits nonnegative horizon-dependent log-volume variance, and samples positive future volumes. Four scratch tests check shrinkage direction, selection, variance fitting, and reproducibility. The module is not yet wired into a patient-fold forecaster; the caller must keep population estimates and time-constant selection inside outer-training data.
- **Track B joint PDE posterior (2026-10-24):** `src/fk_grid_likelihood.py` scores PDE concentration after matching each training mask's volume, using signed-distance residuals at deterministic surface anchors and a supplied spatial Gaussian covariance. `infer_joint_log_parameter_posterior` combines this likelihood with a supplied correlated log-parameter prior and returns joint draws plus posterior mass at parameter bounds. Coarse likelihoods are reused outside a local high-mass refinement. Synthetic tests prefer the generating PDE setting to a wrong diffusion setting. This is not an outer-fold empirical-Bayes runner; callers must provide training-only prior and covariance estimates.
- **Track B hybrid forecast and comparator (2026-10-24):** `src/fk_hybrid_forecast.py` accepts history masks only, combines joint PDE-shape draws with a shrunk log-volume trend and correlated boundary draws, and returns a matched no-PDE signed-distance volume-ranker comparator. It also returns the pre-boundary forecast probability needed to measure held-out residuals after a forecast is frozen. It rejects forecasts that reach computational-domain capacity. It has no patient-data I/O and cannot receive a held-out target mask. The prior, volume-population slope, shrinkage constant, predictive variance, and boundary covariance remain caller-supplied training-only inputs.
