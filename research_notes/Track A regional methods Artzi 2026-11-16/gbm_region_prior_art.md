# Track A direct GBM regional biology prior art

## Which published biological methods already target core, edge, margin, or infiltrated tissue?

### Takeaway

Core-to-edge biology is established. Published work spans histology-guided laser capture, single-cell RNA sequencing, fluorescence-sorted invasive cells, GeoMx/CosMx/Visium spatial profiles, state and niche mapping, paired differential expression, deconvolution, and functional assays. I found no direct study reporting a prespecified, patient-held-out classifier that predicts independent pathology-confirmed primary core versus infiltrative edge with a donor-level score; most papers describe signatures or regional associations.

### Cited Findings

- Ivy GAP links neuropathologist-drawn histologic compartments to laser-microdissected RNA-seq and in-situ hybridization. Its regional labels include leading edge, infiltrating tumor, cellular tumor, pseudopalisading cells around necrosis, and microvascular proliferation. The atlas derives regional gene sets and validates selected markers; it also develops an image-based anatomic-feature annotator, not a patient-held-out RNA region classifier. [Ivy atlas primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6414061/)
- Darmanis et al. profile matched core and peritumoral tissue with single-cell RNA-seq, identify neoplastic cells by expression/CNV evidence, and derive an infiltration-associated transcriptional signature. This is direct tumor-cell biology precedent, but the paper reports discovery across a small paired cohort, not a held-out patient classifier. [Darmanis et al., primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5810554/)
- Yu et al. use MRI-marked tumor and peritumoral sampling points with single-cell expression and patient-level donor-held-out classification. Track A's local frozen protocol identifies the labels as surgical sampling labels, not histologic labels for each cell. [Yu source paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/nsr/nwaa099); [local frozen protocol](../../paper/trackA_yu2020/PROTOCOL.md)
- The edge/core study by Kim et al. contrasts paired MRI-/histology-sampled edge and core tissue, uses PCA and differential-expression heatmaps, and applies Ivy GAP leading-edge and cellular-tumor signatures with ssGSEA. It reports edge/core marker candidates and biological follow-up, not external patient-held-out prediction. [Kim et al., primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-18189-y)
- 5-ALA/FACS studies separate fluorescent invasive-margin cells from non-fluorescent cells and mixed core, rim, and invasive-margin tissue. They use RNA-seq or arrays, differential expression, pathway enrichment, cross-platform checks, and experimental validation of candidates such as SERPINE1. The sorted-cell signature targets a rare 5-ALA-positive population, whereas Artzi's P-IT/P-CT labels denote whole pathologist-selected tissue regions. [Primary 5-ALA study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7462276/); [later spatial 5-ALA study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10337060/)
- Greenwald et al. build within-sample Leiden and NMF gene programs, consolidate cross-sample metaprograms, project them onto Visium, and describe a layered tumor architecture from hypoxic core to infiltrated brain. Layers 4–5 and cell-state signatures are relevant fixed biological comparators, but the study is spatial organization and program discovery, not a held-out core/edge classifier. [Greenwald et al., primary paper and methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11088502/)
- Harwood et al. use CosMx and GeoMx with H&E-selected fields/regions, cell segmentation, cell-neighbor aggregation, NMF/state analysis, and differential expression. They report Notch, synaptic, and glial/neurodevelopmental programs in infiltrated tissue. A later CosMx study uses neuropathologist-reviewed core and infiltrated tissue, single-cell clustering/NMF, neighbor-weighted expression, and pseudo-bulk/limma; its region call for expression analysis also uses malignant-to-resident-cell proportions. These are direct comparators and a warning against treating expression-derived or cell-composition-derived region calls as independent pathology labels. [Harwood 2024 primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11385527/); [Harwood later primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12771808/)
- Pai et al. contrast pathology- and MRI-guided core versus distal margin in grade-4 glioma. They use single-cell and pseudobulk differential expression, repeated cell subsampling, cell-state/pathway scoring, RNA velocity/pseudotime, chromatin accessibility, cell-cell interaction, and TF/regulon analyses. Their shared “glioma infiltration” panel is a direct external fixed-signature comparator; their study does not report a held-out-patient regional predictor. The cohort includes different grade-4 molecular drivers, so an IDH-wildtype-only adaptation needs an explicit rule. [Pai et al., primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12603948/)
- The Petti preprint uses histology-annotated spatial expression, cell-state localization, and niche models along the core-edge axis. It is relevant spatial-method precedent, but the available source is a preprint and the previously audited deposit has unresolved pairing/annotation joins. [Petti preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.04.04.647096v3.full); [local crosswalk audit](../../paper/trackA_alternate_dataset_plan_20261101.md)
- Recent single-cell spatial studies expand the established state/niche family: Harwood's CosMx study uses patient-specific clustering and NMF to distinguish malignant states and reports heterogeneous core-to-infiltration changes; de Jong et al. map snRNA-seq states to Visium with cell2location, infer spatial microenvironments with NMF, and describe malignant-state zonation. Neither is a pathology-label prediction benchmark. [Harwood CosMx primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12771808/); [de Jong et al., primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11234-8)
- The targeted spatial matrisome study uses a 478-gene panel, Ivy GAP regional expression, spatial single-cell profiles, and marker-based regional programs. It supports an ECM/matrisome score as a comparator, but its assay is intentionally selected and its analysis is descriptive/region-specific. [Primary matrisome paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12769532/)
- The MRI-guided multi-sampling study combines regional WES/RNA-seq, deconvolution, and drug testing in patient-derived 3D models. The reported functional drug-response experiments use only two patient-derived models; this is direct spatial drug-vulnerability precedent, not a patient-level clinical response predictor. [Primary multi-sampling paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13550674/)
- Other direct spatial biology includes GeoMx core-edge mixed-effects analysis, histology-associated gene-gradient analysis, and spatially localized 5-ALA/metabolic or neuron/astrocyte interaction programs. These methods support paired/mixed-effects, gradient, and interaction-score comparators where their feature definitions and inputs are available. [GeoMx core-edge abstract](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11553870/); [histology-gradient primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-50904-x); [astrocyte-invasion primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12459425/)
- The 2026 Sonpatki atlas is a particularly close Xenium/Visium comparator omitted from the earlier local note's detail: it combines a curated single-cell reference with whole-transcriptome Visium and Xenium; uses cell markers, composition, spatial proximity, and H&E to assign anatomical features; maps cell-subtype scores and spatial relationships; and validates malignant calls with inferred CNV. It reports 115,914 Visium spots from 32 specimens and a 56-subtype spatial map, with Xenium data from four GBM patients. The feature labels are a joint expression/composition/histology mapping, not an independent patient-held-out region predictor. The fixed cell-subtype programs may be scored on Artzi only after gene coverage is checked; the full method needs the spatial coordinates, tissue image, and reference. Patient overlap with Artzi is unverified. [Sonpatki et al., primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-69716-2)
- A separate Visium multi-cohort study by Ravikumar et al. is a bioRxiv preprint, not a peer-reviewed publication. It scores Ivy GAP regional markers with UCell, labels spots by their highest regional score, integrates spatial slides, deconvolves cell mixture, extracts tumor-specific expression, then uses differential expression, pathway and TF analysis, hdWGCNA, and spatial ligand-receptor analysis. Its data comprise 14 slides from seven new GBM patients, 17 Freiburg slides, and 13 Broad slides, totaling 44 slides and 128,176 spots. It reports niche programs such as Notch in cellular tumor, hypoxia around necrosis, and neuronal signaling at the leading edge. These are spatial discovery analyses without a patient-held-out prediction split; gene sets are portable in principle, while score-defined labels and full spatial analyses are not independent of expression or tissue context. [Ravikumar et al., primary preprint](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.10.17.683110)
- Petry et al. now have a peer-reviewed 2026 article, updating the preprint lead in the earlier inventory. Inputs are multiregional human GBM core, periphery, and infiltrated-cortex snRNA-seq plus spatial transcriptomic validation, followed by patient-derived GBM cells and human cortical-slice perturbation. The reported output is a core-enriched mesenchymal-like malignant subpopulation with co-occurring iron-handling, stress-adaptation, invasion, and angiogenic programs; it tests iron exposure as a functional perturbation. It is a regional biological association and tissue-model experiment, not a region classifier or patient-level drug-response predictor; no patient-held-out evaluation is described in the accessible article record. Its frozen iron/invasion gene sets could be gene-panel comparators if the Xenium panel covers them. [Petry et al., primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/neuonc/noag220)
- A related 2023 study perturbs cellular iron in human GBM cells and reanalyzes an existing human GBM single-cell dataset, finding a peripheral-cell expression pattern consistent with lower cellular iron. The experimental output is migration and polarity, including CDC42 expression; the tissue-region expression result is a post hoc biological association from the existing dataset, not a trained region predictor. This is useful context for the later Petry iron program, but it is not an independent fixed classifier. [Shenoy et al., primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1096/fj.202202157RR)
- Chanoch-Myers et al. profile human-cell PDX models rather than patient tissue: they compare malignant scRNA-seq from tumor core and contralateral brain, use Visium and CODEX to map tumor core, periphery, medial, and contralateral regions, and derive local- and distal-invasion programs. The paper distinguishes invasion propensity in model-level core profiles from active invading-cell programs; its gene sets are direct biological comparators but the labels are model-system invasion locations, not pathology-reviewed human P-CT/P-IT. The study does not report a patient-held-out regional classifier. Frozen gene lists may port if Artzi gene coverage is adequate, but expression-only Artzi cannot reproduce spatial or anatomical validation. [Chanoch-Myers et al., primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-70470-8)
- Kang et al. define tumor bulk, oncoField, oncoInfiltrate, and oncoStream invasion niches in an immunocompetent mouse GBM model, derive malignant and tumor-associated microglia single-cell states, and validate signature localization against Ivy GAP zones and human Visium. This supplies invasion and myeloid-niche biology, including bulk-, stream-, and infiltrate-associated programs, but its discovery labels are mouse-model morphologies and human spatial validation is by signature scoring, not held-out pathology classification. Only frozen cross-species gene sets are candidates for Artzi; the immune-interaction and imaging method cannot be reproduced from the Xenium expression table alone. [Kang et al., primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12353550/)
- Nebeling et al. use longitudinal three-photon intravital microscopy in an autochthonous immunocompetent mouse GBM model to compare far-infiltration-zone microglia and tumor behavior by location, cell density, and tumor-microtube morphology, then perturb CX3CR1 and deplete microglia pharmacologically. Labels are anatomical/distance zones in a mouse model, not human pathologist-labeled regions; outputs are dynamic migration and microglial-response measures, not a gene signature or regional expression classifier. No patient-level split applies. This is mechanistic biological context and cannot be reproduced from Artzi Xenium expression alone. [Nebeling et al., primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2026.03.010)
- GlioTrace is a 2026 preclinical drug-response method, not an expression signature: it combines live confocal movies from patient-derived xenograft brain slices, CNN morphology calls, cell tracking, and a covariate hidden Markov model to estimate phenotype transitions under local tissue and treatment conditions. The morphologies are branching, diffuse translocation, locomotion, perivascular translocation, and round; its image classifier uses leave-one-patient-model-out validation, while drug effects use matched slice controls and model-level experiments. It requires time-lapse images, tissue slices, and perturbations, so it cannot be run on Artzi Xenium data and does not validate patient clinical response. [Berglund et al., primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-78908-9)
- A 2025/2026 AACR abstract reports pathology-annotated core, edge, and infiltrating-zone Visium HD profiling, and later adds spatial proteomics (COMET). It describes transcriptional modules and edge-derived malignant-cell programs in primary and matched recurrent IDH-wildtype cases. This is direct label/task precedent but remains conference-abstract evidence; the abstract does not provide a fixed, reproducible score or a patient-held-out split. It is not yet a benchmarkable Artzi comparator without a full paper and exact feature definitions. [AACR 2026 abstract](https://www.aacr.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/AACR2026_Proceedings_Part-1.pdf)
- A primary longitudinal atlas provides an important confound-control method but does not directly label intratumoral regions: it profiles matched primary and recurrent tumors by snRNA-seq, then compares state-controlled pseudobulk profiles after downsampling each sample to the same number of cells within shared malignant states. The labels are time points and matched donors, not core versus infiltrative edge; this is a paired state-composition control that can inform an Artzi adapter, not a region signature. No patient-held-out region prediction is reported. [GBM CARE primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12081298/)

### Inferences

- “A new GBM core-versus-edge signature” is not a defensible novelty claim. Direct prior art already includes region-specific markers, shared margin programs, cell-state gradients, niche maps, and functional invasion programs.
- Strongest narrow method gap is a frozen predictor evaluated with all samples from a patient kept together, pathology labels as outcome, and one donor-level score shared across candidate and comparators. This is an inference from the outcome/analysis types reported in the cited studies, not proof that no such paper exists.
- Artzi baseline panels should prioritize externally derived, portable signatures: Ivy LE/CT, Darmanis, Kim, 5-ALA-positive invasion, Greenwald Layers 4–5, Harwood Notch/synaptic, Pai glioma-infiltration, and matrisome. Fit any data-derived signature inside training patients only. Include ordinary paired differential-expression and cell-composition controls because several source papers demonstrate strong regional mixture changes.
- Methods using full-transcriptome Visium or multi-omic measurements can be represented only by their frozen gene-set or score when the original input is unavailable. Any method relying on cell2location, tissue imagery, ATAC, spatial coordinates, or cell-state labels needs a modality and leakage audit before inclusion.
- The Artzi dataset metadata audit records 10 primary patient codes with both P-CT and P-IT malignant cells, but does not provide a surgery ID; same-resection pairing remains an inference. It also states no expression matrix was opened. [Local Artzi metadata audit](../../paper/trackA_artzi_zenodo_metadata_2026-11-16.md)

### Gaps

- I did not find evidence that each signature above has a retrievable, version-pinned weight vector with adequate overlap against the Artzi Xenium gene panel. Run a symbol/coverage check only after the evaluation protocol and comparator manifest are frozen.
- Artzi's released malignant/state labels are based on study-level clustering and inferred CNV, as described in the user-supplied paper methods. Using them as if independently assigned per held-out patient would compromise a strict patient-separated analysis. An external frozen mapping or an all-cell primary endpoint is needed; the exact cell-level annotation schema still requires verification.
- The Artzi primary subset may overlap earlier Harwood cohort donors. No patient-level overlap crosswalk was verified in this search. Such overlap matters if a Harwood-derived signature or data are used for development; author/cohort crosswalk or exact coded-ID audit is required before calling any result independent.
- Search was targeted to primary studies on human GBM core, periphery, edge, infiltration, and margin. I did not exhaust every spatial-method paper in other cancers or every MRI/radiomics/optical margin classifier; those are different input/label tasks and need a separately bounded method inventory.

## Which methods can be ported to the Artzi Xenium P-CT/P-IT task?

### Takeaway

Fixed external gene programs and standard expression classifiers are the most portable candidates. Spatial graph and deconvolution methods are possible only if required coordinates, cell labels, reference matrices, and gene coverage are verified without leaking held-out patients; papers that infer region from expression or mixture proportion cannot supply independent target labels.

### Cited Findings

- The source PDF says Artzi uses pathology-guided H&E region selection with neuropathologist confirmation to label P-CT and P-IT tissue, followed by targeted Xenium counts and study-specific cell annotation. The paper analyzes regional ecosystems and cell states; it does not report a patient-held-out P-CT/P-IT predictor. Its malignant-cell pipeline aggregates meta-cells, clusters across samples, infers CNV using pooled references, and assigns cell-level malignancy from those pooled results. Those study-wide calls cannot be carried into a strict patient-held-out test as independent input labels. [Artzi article, user-supplied PDF, Methods pp. 17 and 20–23](C:/Users/vihan/Downloads/cd-25-2302.pdf)
- Artzi's Zenodo release metadata audit reports the available P-CT/P-IT patient overlap and the missing specimen-level surgery identifier; expression was not opened during that audit. [Local Artzi metadata audit](../../paper/trackA_artzi_zenodo_metadata_2026-11-16.md)
- The Harwood follow-up directly compares spatial expression and uses cell-neighbor aggregation and region-specific pseudo-bulk/limma, but its region analysis includes cell-proportion-derived assignment. [Harwood primary methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12771808/)
- Pai's independent fixed gene signature was projected to spatial core/transition/margin regions, with expression scores used to check spatial localization; the underlying label was pathology/RNA region annotation. [Pai et al., primary methods/results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12603948/)
- Ivy GAP supports gene-set transfer from histology-defined regions to new profiles and distinguishes LE, IT, CT, PAN, and MVP through reference expression data. [Ivy GAP primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6414061/)
- Sonpatki's human spatial atlas uses both Visium and Xenium and supplies fixed cell-subtype and niche programs; those scores can be ported only after target-panel coverage is established. Its full anatomical-feature mapping also uses H&E, cell composition, and spatial proximity, so it is not an expression-only held-out comparator. Petry's regional iron/invasion programs are another candidate gene-set comparator; their functional slice assay cannot be ported. [Sonpatki et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-69716-2); [Petry et al.](https://doi.org/10.1093/neuonc/noag220)
- The published GlioTrace drug-response method requires live imaging, tissue slices, and experimental perturbations, so Artzi Xenium expression cannot reproduce its phenotype or drug-response outputs. The closest defensible comparison would be biological context only, not a shared expression score. [GlioTrace primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-78908-9)
- Without using pooled Artzi test labels to fit a model, fixed source-defined scores are portable in principle: Ivy LE-versus-CT, Darmanis infiltration, Kim edge-versus-core markers, 5-ALA invasive-cell, Greenwald layer/state, Harwood Notch/synaptic, Pai infiltration, matrisome/ECM, and Petry iron/invasion programs. Score each held-out specimen independently from a frozen gene list and predeclared scoring rule; do not use Artzi's pooled cell states or CNV-derived labels to select genes, calibrate thresholds, or filter held-out cells. Harwood and Sonpatki cohort overlap is not verified, so signatures from either remain provisional for an independence claim until a source-cohort crosswalk is checked. [Ivy GAP](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6414061/); [Darmanis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5810554/); [Kim](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-18189-y); [5-ALA invasive-cell study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7462276/); [Greenwald](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11088502/); [Harwood](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11385527/); [Pai](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12603948/); [matrisome study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12769532/); [Petry](https://doi.org/10.1093/neuonc/noag220)
- Any score whose gene list, cell filter, threshold, or feature weights are learned from Artzi labels must be fit inside each outer training fold only. Frozen external signatures that might overlap the Artzi or Harwood source cohort should not be presented as independent external validation until sample overlap is excluded; if overlap cannot be resolved, label those comparisons exploratory. [Artzi article methods](C:/Users/vihan/Downloads/cd-25-2302.pdf); [Harwood later spatial study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12771808/)

### Inferences

- A fair Artzi benchmark can use one patient-level target and one paired concordance score for all algorithms; each score may use its native feature type, but all samples from one patient must remain in one outer fold.
- Fixed external signatures are directly portable in principle, subject to predeclared gene mapping and coverage. The source-study weights are not independent if they were discovered in overlapping Artzi/Harwood patients.
- The original Artzi malignant-cell restriction is not an independent predictor input unless its calls are reproduced with a frozen external method or on each held-out patient without pooled test-patient fitting.
- Artzi metadata and pathology can support a whole-tissue region prediction question, but that estimates ecosystem-plus-tumor expression. It cannot, by itself, establish malignant-cell-intrinsic biology.

### Gaps

- No test expression or assay matrix was accessed in this prior-art pass. No gene coverage, signature score, prediction, or patient outcome was computed.
- The study's code is available only upon request according to its data/code statements; source-specific reproduction details beyond the published methods remain unavailable. [Artzi article data/code availability](https://aacrjournals.org/cancerdiscovery/article/doi/10.1158/2159-8290.CD-25-2302/788658/Genomic-alterations-and-treatment-define)
- Confirm primary-sample pairing and donor overlap before designating Artzi an independent external validation cohort.

## What does prior art imply for the novelty claim?

### Takeaway

A method can be new only at a narrow algorithmic level. The biological premise and major regional signatures already exist. Any novelty claim must distinguish the candidate's exact training objective and test design from the existing paired, robust, rank-based, state-balanced, and spatial methods.

### Cited Findings

- Published studies already use within-patient core-edge contrasts, patient-aware/mixed-effect models, pseudobulk differential expression, cross-sample signature aggregation, state balancing, gene-set scoring, spatial neighbors, and cell-type deconvolution. [Ivy GAP](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6414061/); [Greenwald](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11088502/); [Harwood](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12771808/); [Pai](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12603948/); [GeoMx core-edge analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11553870/)
- The Artzi article itself uses a study-derived malignant cell annotation and NMF-based states, so reusing its post hoc state labels as “new method” features would restate source-paper analysis rather than establish a novel predictor. [Artzi article methods, user-supplied PDF pp. 20–23](C:/Users/vihan/Downloads/cd-25-2302.pdf)

### Inferences

- Novelty should be claimed, if at all, for a precise algorithmic rule and patient-held-out validation design, not for detecting that core and infiltrative edge differ.
- A stronger margin signature or a positive score on Artzi alone cannot establish a new method. It requires a predeclared method, the full applicable comparator set, and no post-score tuning.
- Because this dataset was selected after earlier negative Track A results and the sample pairing/overlap gates remain open, a first run would be exploratory even if its analysis plan is frozen before expression access.

### Gaps

- This search does not establish worldwide priority or prove that no exact robust paired classifier exists. A separate methods search must check gene-pair methods, matched-pair feature selection, spatial graph classifiers, and domain-generalization methods before declaring an algorithm unique.
- The source papers mostly lack patient-held-out predictor reports. Their published biological scores can be compared, but they do not all have an original sample-level prediction rule; adapters must be declared before any Artzi expression values are viewed.
