2ae. **Selling split makes the positivity-preserving solver exact (L1, `src/solver_monotone.py` `split="selling"`, `output/solver_verification_monotone.json` now has `split: selling`, 2026-10-08).** The NNLS split of 2ac was not exact where a tensor is not a non-negative sum of its 37 offsets.
